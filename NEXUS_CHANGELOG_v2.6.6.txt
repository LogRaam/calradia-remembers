Version 2.6.6

Three additions from your suggestions.

New

- Letters now build regard. Before, you could exchange a dozen letters with someone and still meet them as near-strangers. Now the person reading your letter judges it: a warm letter raises their regard by a point, at most once every 5 days per correspondent (faster on the warmer relation settings). Letters alone take you as far as friendly regard; beyond that, you have to meet in person. A rude or insulting letter costs a point. (Thank you, Lordfadooboo.)
- The game's own mercenary companies are now judged like yours. A company that leaves a realm in the middle of a losing war, or goes straight over to its enemy, is resented by that realm's lords, and word of it gets about. Ending a contract in peace, or with the war won, is not news. (Thank you, Zivkyn and fkasad.)
- Leaving a realm as a vassal with no fief is judged like leaving a mercenary contract, except that you swore an oath: abandoning a losing war or going over to the enemy weighs twice as much, three times for a realm of your own people, and you are never thanked for leaving. A vassal who leaves with their fiefs takes them to war against the realm, and that war is the consequence. (Thank you, fkasad.)

Notes

- Your existing saves load as they are.
