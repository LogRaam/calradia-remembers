## The scene
**Arwa** *(to Garios)*: The camp stores were raided last night. Two loaves and a whole ham. Who keeps watch on them?

> *Bryn shifts from one foot to the other.*

**Garios** *(to Bryn)*: *rounding on him with a sharp glare* What is this fidgeting, squire? If you have knowledge of the watch or the missing stores, speak it now and cease shaming my house.

**Bryn** *(to Garios)*: *I shrink from his glare, eyes fixed on the dirt, voice a frightened whisper* I... I cannot say, my lord. Forgive this silence.

**Garios** *(to Bryn)*: *My gauntlet cracks across his jaw, sending him staggering.* Then you will bleed for your silence until the truth spills out. Arwa, have the guards fetch the whip.

> *Bryn clasps his hands tightly as Ira curls her lip.*

**Arwa** *(to Garios)*: *raising a hand to halt any guards* Enough, my lord. Your house's honor will not be mended by breaking a boy's jaw before the truth is even asked. Ira, walk with me and the squire, let us hear what the night truly held.

> *Garios clenches his fists as Bryn's lip quivers.*

**Ira** *(to Arwa)*: *cutting across Garios with a flat stare* Aye, commander, I'll walk with you. *jerking my chin at Bryn* Boy looks ready to piss himself; best get answers before his lord cracks the other side of his face.

> *Bryn's hands tremble at his sides.*

**Garios** *(to Arwa)*: *My voice cuts sharp through the air, fists still balled at my sides* The squire bears my colors, Arwa; you will not spirit him off with this filth to whisper behind my back. Bring him before me here or I will fetch the guards myself and let the whole camp see how my house deals in shame.

> *Bryn swallows hard.*

**Arwa** *(to Garios)*: The camp answers to me, not your house's temper. *stepping between him and the boy, voice level but carrying* Bryn, you had the watch. What passed at the stores last night? Speak it plain and you have my protection.

> *Garios grinds his teeth.*

**Bryn** *(to Arwa)*: *I flinch at my lord's grinding teeth, edging half behind you with my gaze locked on the dirt* Commander... my lord's honor rests on my tongue. I dare not loose it here.

**Garios** *(to Bryn)*: *My eyes bore into the boy like daggers, voice dropping to a venomous hiss* Then you will unburden it to your lord this instant, squire, or I will cast you from my service and let the shame be yours alone. No one shields my name but me.

**Bryn** *(to Garios)*: *I drop to one knee, shaking so hard my teeth nearly chatter, voice barely above a whisper while my eyes stay glued to the ground* My lord, your honor is all I have. Grant me your ear alone and I will unburden what I can.

**Arwa** *(to Garios)*: *turning my head just enough to meet Garios's eyes while keeping the boy in my shadow* Take him behind your tent then, but you have the time it takes me to walk the eastern picket and back. Bring me the truth of my stolen stores when you return, or I will finish this inquiry myself.

> *Bryn digs his fingernails into his palms.*

**Garios** *(to Arwa)*: *My glare flicks from the kneeling boy to Arwa, voice tight with barely leashed fury* As you command, then. The time is short and my patience shorter. *I grip Bryn's shoulder like a vice and shove him ahead of me toward the tent* Walk, squire.

> *Bryn's knees knock together.*

**Arwa** *(to Ira)*: *turning from the pair as they retreat toward the tent, I fall into step toward the eastern picket* Walk with me, captain. The boy is terrified of more than a lord's temper; tell me what your sellswords' eyes saw last night.

> *Bryn wrings his hands.*

**Ira** *(to Arwa)*: *matching your pace with a loose shrug* My blades saw shadows slipping near the stores, but they weren't ours. Ask me what that boy's really hiding from his lord and I'll wager it's not a mercenary knife at his back.

> *Bryn's eyes widen.*

**Arwa** *(to Ira)*: *keeping stride along the picket, voice low* Then tell me plain what you think the boy hides from his lord. A ham and two loaves are poor stakes for such terror, and I mean to have the truth before Garios breaks him worse.

> *Bryn covers his face with both hands.*

**Ira** *(to Arwa)*: *scowling at the distant tent flap* Boy's shaking like he's the one who opened the stores himself, maybe to settle some debt his lord don't know about. If Garios breaks him for it we'll never see the ham or the truth; I'd sooner question the lad without that gauntlet in his face.

> *Bryn's breath catches in his throat.*

**Arwa** *(to Ira)*: *halting our walk with a sharp turn back toward the tent* Then we end the waiting. The boy sounds ready to shatter; I will pull him under my shield now and hear it all before Garios's temper does more harm. Come.

> *Bryn draws his arms tightly around his chest.*

**Garios** *(to Arwa)*: *striding out from behind the tent with Bryn hauled tight beside me, voice cracking like a whip* Your pity ends here, commander; the boy has yielded what honor permits me to hear and no more. *I shove him forward roughly* Take your mercenary and quit circling my house's shame.

> *Bryn hunches his shoulders.*

**Ira** *(to Arwa)*: *planting myself between Garios and the boy, arms crossed* The lad's already half-broken and his lord's "honor" explains nothing. Give him to me for an hour without the gauntlets, commander; I'll fetch the truth and your ham before this puffed-up ass turns him into a corpse.

> *Bryn wipes his palms on his tunic.*

**Garios** *(to Arwa)*: *My fingers dig deeper into Bryn's shoulder, yanking him fully behind me as I bare my teeth at the mercenary* Your captain's tongue drips with insolence I will not stomach, commander. The boy's words are mine alone; compensate your stores from the next levy and end this farce before blood follows words.

> *Bryn bites his lower lip.*
