## Cohesion: how many parts each namespace falls into

Only the links between a namespace's OWN types are kept (one type's file names the other). A namespace in one
part is one module. Several parts are several modules sharing a folder; types on their own are linked to no
sibling at all (often fine for a data type or a Harmony patch, worth a look for a service). Density is the share
of possible sibling links that exist: tight (30% or more), medium (10% or more), loose. Namespaces of 4 types or more.
In Logic, a type on its own is the design (each pure rule stands alone and the mod calls it); there, the PARTS
count is what to read: a namespace of many unrelated parts is a folder of things that could each have their own.
