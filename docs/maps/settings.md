# Settings: who obeys each MCM switch

GENERATED by the Maps fixtures in `CalradiaRemembers.Tests/Maps`. Do not edit. A test fails when this file
no longer matches the code; see `docs/maps/README.md`.

A switch obeyed by one road and not by another that does the same thing has shipped three times ("Arrests and
outlaws" off and the hunters still rode; adult content off and the NSFW model still took over; "NPCs Raise World
News" off and companions still brought news). **Before adding a road that does what a setting governs (a new way to
approach, to write, to pick a model), find the setting here and make the new road one of its readers.** When a
player says "I turned it off and it still happens", the readers listed here are the roads that obey it: the one doing
it is missing from the list. Read from syntax; blind spots in the header of `SettingsReadersMap.cs` (a value handed
on to the SDK or a helper shows only where it is read).

119 settings; a reader is `Type.Member`. Console and cr.test reads are counted apart (they exercise a switch, they do not obey it).
