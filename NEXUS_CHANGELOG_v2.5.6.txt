Version 2.5.6

Your saves from 2.5.5 carry over untouched. Every fix here came from a player's own log. Thank you for sending them.

Fixed
- A chat that fails to open no longer kills every conversation until you restart the game. This is the important one. It was reported like this: "first time everything works, then if I select 'A word with you' on a lord the chat panel closes on its own straight away. It doesn't hang or throw anything visible, it just isn't there. The conversation never starts." The mod decided whether a chat was already open from a single piece of bookkeeping, so if one ever opened without being properly closed, that bookkeeping stayed stuck, every later attempt quietly did nothing, and the ordinary dialogue, having nowhere to go, simply ended. It now checks whether the window is actually still there, clears the leftover if it is not, and opens normally. If you were restarting the game to get your conversations back, that should be over.
- Succeeding at a rescue no longer cancels the quest to rescue. Reported: "I got the person out of prison but then a couple of days later I get 'so and so is lost, they are no longer on the map' and the quest is failed." The mod only recognised a rescue if you won a battle against the party carrying the prisoner, so breaking someone out of a dungeon counted for nothing, and the daily check then saw a captive who was no longer captive and concluded the task had become impossible. Your rescue is now recognised however you managed it, whether you staged a prison break or took the whole settlement. And on the rare occasion a task really is set aside because someone else freed them first, it says so, instead of telling you they vanished from the world.
- Kings and Emperors know that they rule. Reported: "I tried to address Derthert and Garios as kings but they deny the fact and claim that they are just a lord serving the throne. This is pretty annoying when you try to do diplomacy via dialogue." They were not being modest, they had simply never been told. Every ruler now knows their own crown, and knows it by their realm's own word for it, so an Emperor is an Emperor and a Khan is a Khan. Clan heads know they lead their house, too.
- A character no longer starts the conversation over at every reply. If you had just heard a wanderer's life story through the ordinary dialogue, that exchange was handed to the AI as a conversation still awaiting answers, and since it opens with you introducing yourself, the AI kept answering the introduction: greeting you again, and sometimes introducing itself all over again. That exchange is now passed along as what it always was, a bridge from the ordinary dialogue into the chat.
- You pay the price that was named. A companion who had just said "five hundred and ninety-five denars" counted the coin aloud as "five... ninety-five", and the game received 95, which an old rule then pulled up to 446. So the sum that changed hands was one nobody had ever said. What is agreed in conversation is now charged exactly, and if your purse cannot cover it the deal simply does not close.
- Nineteen mod options no longer run off the edge of the screen. These are written as "On (default): ... Off: ...", so the half that scrolled out of view was usually the one explaining the other position. All of them now fit.

New
- Take your own people aside before you speak. Ask a lord, in the ordinary dialogue, for a moment with your own companions, and choose who joins you. Tell them what you want from the meeting to come and they answer as themselves: they can question the plan, or tell you plainly they are the wrong person for it. What you told them travels with them into the conversation that follows, and the lord never learns a word of it. You can go back and brief them again if you forgot something.
- Companions ask for a word while travelling. They would only ever do it when you entered a settlement, so if you spend your time on the road, they never spoke up at all. There is a new mod option, "Companions Asking For A Word", from Never to Often, if you want more of them or none.

Changed
- A companion confides their feelings sooner if you asked for a warmer game. How deep a bond it takes before they say anything now follows your Relationship Pacing setting: unchanged on Grounded, and within reach on Warm or Storyteller.

A note on the two new things above: they were built today from your suggestions and they have not been played much. If either misbehaves, turn on logging in the mod options, reproduce it once, and send the log. The mod writes a great deal about what it is doing, and every fix in this release was found that way.
