Version 2.6.0

Two new pieces this time. Letters are never lost for good, but a letter taken on the road can betray you. And, experimental and off by default, arrests in the King's name: warrants, trials before a council, outlaws and brigands.

New

- A lost letter is sent again until it arrives. When a courier is taken or lost on the road, the letter goes again with a guarded courier, in both directions, and you are told what really happened to the first one. The one who wrote it remembers writing it, and knows their courier never arrived, instead of making up an excuse when you ask. (Thank you, raphareish.)

- What a stolen letter said travels in the world. Bandits who take a courier carry the letter off. Whoever beats them sends it up to the lord they answer to (a town's patrol to that town's owner), and a band that goes home leaves it in its hideout. The lord who reads it remembers what it said, word gets about, and they react to it as it deserves: a plot against them is not forgotten. Hunt the band down yourself, or clear its hideout, and you take your letter back unread.

- Arrests in the King's name (experimental, OFF by default: turn on "Arrests and outlaws" in the mod options). As a vassal or mercenary, a grave act (a letter of yours read as treason, violence against your own realm, a severe crime rating) brings a warrant, and sergeants ride out to find you.
  Surrender, and you are put in your liege's cells and tried before a council: your liege and the lords of the realm who hate you most. Speak for yourself; a bench you win over may acquit you, and then every charge is dropped. Convicted, you serve your sentence and are released.
  Refuse, fight the sergeants off, or break out, and you are an outlaw: you leave the realm, there is a price on your head, and the realm makes war on you. Bounty hunters come after you; if you are taken, you are handed over and tried. A realm may, rarely, declare an amnesty.
  Attacking a caravan, a merchant ship or a fishing boat in peace makes you a brigand: the realm that sent a merchant and the realm it was bound for both want you.
  A kingdom's ruler is never arrested: for them it is politics and war.
  These have not yet been played through at length. Reports are very welcome.

Changed

- The "Intercepted letters can leak" option is gone: a stolen letter that says something worth hiding now always travels with its captors, as described above. "Invulnerable Couriers" remains the one switch to turn interception off.

- Councils have more room to answer, so a model that thinks before it speaks no longer runs out of room and leaves the table silent.

For modders

- character_overrides.json: an entry's id can now be the TEMPLATE a hero spawns from (for example a mercenary captain's spc_..._leader_0), so it applies in every campaign even though the hero's own id and name are rolled anew each time. (Thank you, tashmetu.)

- cr.lore_status now lists the prompt files read from your Custom folder, warns when the shipped version is newer than your copy, and says whether world.txt is sent at all (the Compact prompt leaves it out).

Developer tools

- cr.testall: new self-tests for the courier secrets and the arrests.

Notes

- Your existing saves load as they are.
