Version 2.5.5

Your saves from 2.5.4 carry over untouched. Everything here came from player reports this week.

Fixed
- Group conversations keep their thread. When you turned from one person to another, the one you turned to saw a room full of people with no past: everyone's memory of what had been said and agreed was quietly dropped, and only the character you started with ever kept theirs. Since a group conversation is precisely the act of addressing one person and then another, this was the case that mattered most. It is fixed at the root, in a way that cannot come back: there is now one single path that hands out those memories, so no future change can forget half of them.
- If you run a small local model, nobody remembered anything at all. The compact prompt, the one the mod itself tells you to enable for a 4k to 8k model, was dropping every bystander's memory outright. Two people in the room now keep a short recall, companions first, trimmed so that a party of eight costs no more than a party of two.

Changed
- Whether a character is polyamorous now comes from their CULTURE, not from being dishonourable. Until now the only way to reach it was a negative Honor trait, which quietly treated a way of loving as a moral failing, and it ignored jealousy_cultures.json completely, even though the mod already reads that file to decide which cultures tolerate shared partners and which keep harems. The result was that a noble of a harem culture came out strictly monogamous unless he happened to be a scoundrel. Honour still decides what actually belongs to it: infidelity, meaning the betrayal of an exclusivity that was genuinely promised. (Raised by a player who read the code and asked the right question.)
- The same for a taste for brief, unattached romance. It now follows how much a character gives of themselves rather than their honour, so an honourable person who simply keeps their distance can exist, which the old rule made impossible.
- Both changes only affect characters whose romantic profile has not been generated yet, so nobody in your current campaign will change.

New
- You can now write a character's romantic preferences by hand in character_overrides.json, the same way you can already set their orientation and their kinks. Add "preferences": ["Polyamorous"] to an entry (the full list of names is documented in the file's own header) to write one character against the grain of their culture. Leave the field out and the usual roll stands; an empty list is a deliberate lock to none. As with the other authored fields, a mistyped name is skipped rather than crashing anything, and is then named for you by cr.lore_status and in the reload log, so a hand-edited file never fails silently. (Requested by a player.)
