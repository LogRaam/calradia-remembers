Version 2.7.10

Bargains and strangers: you can sell what you carry, a captive can name her own price, and an invitation tells you who is asking.

New

- Selling an item in a conversation is now one deal: the item goes, the buyer pays you, and you confirm the item and the price first ("Sell X to Y for N coins? [Y/n]"), never more than the buyer can pay. Hold the item out with the bag button and the buyer knows what it is worth and what their own purse can afford before naming a price; a sale haggled over several replies keeps that item on the table. (Thank you, 3chmidt.)
- A captive you hold can bargain for her freedom herself. She knows what she can really offer (a ransom her house can pay, her house's fealty to the realm you serve, her service, and in Mature content and above, her bed) and how readily she bargains: an honorless captive makes the first offer instead of daring you to kill her; an honorable one may wait for a proper ransom and never sells herself. Her offer is put to you to accept or refuse, and accepting carries it out and sets her free. (Thank you, Maifailun.)
- Every invitation to talk says who is asking: their house and culture, their rank (ruler, head of a house, a lord of a realm, a merchant of a town...), whether you have met, and how they regard you, in the same words as their encyclopedia page. A "Who is this?" button opens their encyclopedia page; close it and the same invitation comes back. (Thank you, Lordfadooboo and fkasad.)
- For a local model: Mod Options, LLM Provider, "Local model context (tokens)". Set it to your server's context length and CR fits each request to it itself (the oldest lines of the conversation first, then the compact prompt), instead of the server cutting the middle of the prompt, where the rules for actions sit. If even the compact prompt does not fit, you are told once. 0, the default, changes nothing for cloud models. (Thank you, fkasad.)
- Mod Options: a prompt profile to choose, for players and mods who write their own prompts (see For modders).

Fixed

- A gift you write in words, without the bag, now asks first and names the item the game would really take, so a second sword can no longer slip out of your inventory. (Thank you, 3chmidt.)
- After a conversation with a friendly lord on the map, you could be left on the "You have encountered..." menu, as if a battle were under way. Only a fight really going on now keeps that menu. (Thank you, Wallace1067.)
- Battle memories no longer read "at at the siege of...", and the lines a companion keeps of your deeds name you ("I saw Chief Can cut down 15 of them") instead of "you", which the character could take for themselves. (Thank you, tashmetu.)

Changed

- An envoy sent again about a prisoner you hold comes with a better offer, and fewer envoys come altogether. (Thank you, fkasad.)

For modders

- Written tales and scripted scenarios answer each other: every stage and ending of a tale fires ON_TALE:<tale>:<stage> / :end:<ending> with the tale's people; a rule can name them by role ({giver}, {husband}, {rival}, {player}); IF_RULES takes one argument; PRESS_FOR_WAR has a lord push his ruler toward war. A tale can be given by a married lady of another realm (requires.giver married / not_clan_leader / not_of_players_realm), bind her husband as {husband}, and end with "elope" (she leaves him and rides with the player, who may later marry her) or "return_spouse" (she goes back; if her husband is on campaign she goes home and the wedding waits). HOWTO_Quests.md and the Scenario Composer explain it.
- A tale's picture name may carry the giver's realm: "queen_{giver_realm}.jpg".
- Characters with a chosen face: a faces file (your Custom/faces.json, or a mod's ModuleData/cr_faces.json) dresses each realm's queen in a face made in the character editor, with an optional chat portrait. CR ships no faces of its own in this version. EXTENSION_API.md explains it.
- Prompt profiles: a mod or a player can ship a profile (ModuleData/cr_prompts, or Custom/Prompts) that orders the prompt's sections per kind of character and rewords a section (sections/<key>.txt), within the locks CR keeps on the sections that must stay. This is an early version: a guide comes with the profile editor.
- New verbs in the catalogue: sell_item and counter_offer.

Developer tools

- cr.testall now arranges what each test needs (a disposable companion, a marriage, a lord in a given state) instead of skipping it on a new game, and puts it all back. Tests that would need a real war, peace or change of realm arrange it only in 'cr.test full', on a throwaway save.
- New cr.testall tests: audience_who, sell_item, captive_terms, captive_counter_offer, local_context, close_settled_battle, battle_memory_wording, prompt_profile, realm_faces, tale_husband, tale_elopement, tale_scenario_bridge.
- cr.prompt_check lists the prompt profile in use and what it changes.

Notes

- Your existing saves load as they are.
