Version 2.7.5

Town duels that no longer crash, captors and wives who know what happened, captures framed as the acts of war they are, and new doors for modders.

Fixed

- Taking the field for a duel in a town, castle or village crashed the game while the square loaded. The square now waits until the conversation is truly over before opening. (Thank you, Thoragoros1.)
- After a town duel, the conversation that follows now takes place where your opponent actually stands (the square, the tavern, the hall), with them there in 3D, and goes on from the duel instead of the game's own greeting. Talks with notables in their town benefit too.
- Closing a conversation with a lord whose party was in a fight on the map crashed the game. A battle under way is now left to the game's own menu. (Thank you, Maifailun.)
- A lord who takes you prisoner now remembers how: the battle, where, and the men you led. Your captor will no longer take you for a lone spy. (Thank you, Windmolen.)
- Your partners now know each other: a wife knows your other partners by name, and a relative who is also your partner (her mother, say) is known as such, letters included. (Thank you, Windmolen.)

Changed

- Taking a defeated lord captive is now told as the ordinary act of war it is: what would truly offend is how a captive is kept. The captive's kin still feel it as their own, his realm as a loss in war, and each lord's nature colours the rest. Ransoms and the captive's grudges are unchanged. (Thank you, Fakade.)
- The lord who gave you one of the game's own quests now remembers it: when you took it on, and when you did it, failed it or let it lapse. (Thank you, Maifailun and tashmetu.)
- A conversation no longer opens by asking which companions come along: companions truly there join by themselves, and the Invite button brings in anyone else. "Ask Which Companions Come Along" (MCM, Content) brings the old question back.
- A gesture a character wrote twice in one reply is shown once. (Thank you, tashmetu.)

For modders

- New memory kind "OwnLife": a character's own life apart from the player (their attachments, grudges, affairs with others), kept apart from their history with the player. "Other" means history with the player: do not use it for these. The note an heir reads now frames only the history with their predecessor.
- CrCultures.MarriageCustom(culture, clanTier): how strictly a culture holds marriage, read from CR's jealousy cultures.
- CrRomance.WouldAcceptIntimacy(npcId) reads CR's own consent bar; CrRomance.RecordSharedIntimacy(npcId, summary, mod) records a night from another mod as if it had happened in the chat, and is refused when the chat would not have allowed it.
- EXTENSION_API.md documents all of it. (Thank you, tashmetu.)

Developer tools

- cr.duel_take_field: with a conversation open, the character takes up your challenge, the way a real one does.
- New cr.testall tests: capture_memory, partners_know_each_other, native_quest_memory, romance_api_held_to_the_chats_bar.

Notes

- Your existing saves load as they are.
