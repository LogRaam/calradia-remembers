Version 2.5.1

Your saves from 2.5.0 carry over untouched.

New
- Lords who seek you out now speak of the RIGHT thing. When a lord rides out to find you, he raises the freshest matter he actually knows: a battle you fought side by side, word of your latest deed, or news of the realm he wants your read on. He frames it the way he knows it, as his own recollection of something he lived, or as hearsay he only heard, and he asks about realm gossip rather than pretending to know more than he does. He raises each matter only once, so he never nags you twice about the same thing, and if a chase ends in a letter instead of a meeting, the letter speaks of that same matter rather than a vague "your recent deeds".
- A secret-affair drama, woven through the whole world. If you take a secret lover (a devoted companion, or a married lord you have quietly drawn into an affair), whispers of the bond's existence, though never of who it is, begin to circulate after a while, and other lords grow curious and probe you about it, sometimes without ever guessing that the answer is you. The identity is never deduced: a lord learns who your secret lover is only if you tell him yourself. Once a lord knows, it matters who he is to you: a friend keeps your confidence and may quietly let you know it is safe with him, a rival lets the secret slip to his own friends over time, and an enemy will hold it over you, hinting at what it would cost you and pressing you to pay for his silence.
- Progressive regard: warmth is earned, not farmed. Building someone's regard now takes more than a quick hello. Each point of warmth you earn raises the bar for the next: the first comes easily, but climbing higher asks for several genuine, warm moments within a real conversation, so a long and heartfelt talk moves the needle where a drive-by no longer does. You can no longer farm goodwill by leaving and returning after skipping a few hours; the difficulty eases only with real time, one step at a time. A new MCM setting, "Regard Warmth Cooldown (days)" (default 7), controls how fast it eases (set it to 0 to turn the cooldown off). Intimate moments and falling-outs work exactly as before.

Fixed
- Lords gathering into an army, or already caught up in a battle, no longer break off to ride after you and send those useless "I am entering your lands" letters. A lord only rides out to intercept you when he is genuinely free to.
- A companion no longer offers you a private word over top of a vanilla conversation when you enter a settlement, so the two no longer collide.
- Your chosen narrative style now truly reshapes how characters write to you. Switching the voice changes the length, rhythm, and interiority of what you read, instead of barely changing anything at all.
- The Balance of Power panel and the letter book now fit your screen properly, scaling to its aspect ratio instead of being cropped or mis-zoomed when the ratio changes.

For mod authors
- An external action registered through the modding surface can now carry a short recap that the NPC voices in the same reply, and a mod can prompt an NPC to answer a line (ActionResult.Recap and CrConversation.PromptReply).
