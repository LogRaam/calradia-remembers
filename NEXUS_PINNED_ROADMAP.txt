[NEXT] THE MOOD OF THE DAY (this is the "mood randomizer" from the old list, and it grew)

   Three people have now told me the same thing: too many lords talk the same way. So a
   character will have a mood - but drawn from their own situation rather than at random. A
   lord who has just lost a fief cannot wake up cheerful. Wounds, empty coffers, the news from
   his realm, the hour and the weather all decide what kind of day he is allowed to be having.
   Two conversations with the same man on different days stop being interchangeable.

   His TRAITS decide how the mood comes out, so the same mood is two different men: a
   calculating lord is dry and barbed where a bold one is loud and open. That is the part that
   should fix "they all sound alike", without anyone writing a single line of custom text.

   You will be able to shift it while you talk. Mood moves easily - that is what makes it a
   mood - so a joke, a kindness, or good news warms a guarded man inside one conversation,
   and a threat cools him. How you OPEN a conversation will matter.

   And wit. A lord's wit is a mark of quality, so it will be rare, dry, understated, and
   always at his own expense rather than anyone else's - never a gag, never a pun. Once in a
   long while, and worth remembering when it lands.

   It will reach letters too. A letter is written on a day as well, and a note dashed off by a
   man having a bleak one should not read like the same man at a feast.

WHICH MODEL DO YOU USE? I keep being asked which model is best for roleplay and I genuinely do
not have a complete answer - it depends on your provider, your budget and your context size.
If you have found one you like, say so below with a line about why. One thing I can offer from
my own testing: judge a model on whether it USES the facts the mod hands it, not on how
agreeable it is. A model that refuses you because it noticed you have only just met is doing
its job better than one that says yes to everything.
