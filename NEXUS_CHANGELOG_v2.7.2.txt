Version 2.7.2

A small release: what players asked for since 2.7.1. Written tales are still experimental and OFF by default (MCM, Content, "Authored Quests (experimental)").

Changed

- The chat header now shows a character's own relation with you when it differs from their clan's: "Clan -61 · Own +10 · Personal 0". With True Relations and similar mods, each noble has a relation of their own, and the character already spoke from it; now you can see it too. Without such a mod, the header is as before. (Thank you, Fakade.)

Fixed

- A model whose reasoning cannot be turned off (Gemini 3.7 Flash, for one) refused every action-reading call, so nothing a character did was recorded. Such a refusal is now answered by asking again with the model's own reasoning, and the reply lands. (Thank you, Thoragoros1.)
- When a tale's ending set two lords at odds, the banner said the lord held a grievance "against you". It now names the lord it is held against, and says "you" only when it is you. (Thank you, tashmetu.)

For modders

- Written tales can ask for a giver and a village of a culture: "culture": "battania", or a list ["battania", "sturgia"], on "giver" and on "village". The id or the name works, in any case. The Tale Editor has the field, and HOWTO_Quests.md documents it. (Thank you, tashmetu.)
- A memory a mod injects can now be of kind "Hearsay": news the character only heard. It is shown apart from what they lived with you, never takes the place of a lived memory in the Compact prompt (which shows the two latest heard ones beside its six lived ones), and is never kept by compression just for being recent. A character who has only heard of you still meets you for the first time. EXTENSION_API.md also states that AddMemory has no daily quota, unlike Feed.Record (20 a day per mod). (Thank you, tashmetu.)

Notes

- Your existing saves load as they are.
