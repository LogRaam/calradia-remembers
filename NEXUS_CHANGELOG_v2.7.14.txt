Version 2.7.14

Your language from the first line, and companions' news that is fresh.

Fixed

- Characters now speak your language from their very first line. A character who came to you on the map, and every council, used to open in English even with "Reply Language" set; both now follow it from the start. (Thank you, RaveN017Mod.)
- "Reply Language" left blank now uses the game's own language: a game in French gets French replies, letters and memories without setting anything. With the game in English, nothing changes: characters still answer in whatever language you write. Name a language in the setting to force it, as before. (Thank you, RaveN017Mod.)
- A companion no longer comes to tell you old or overtaken news. They bring only news of the past week, still true, that you did not live yourself: no siege told after the castle has fallen, no war of your own realm announced as if from afar. They say how long ago it happened, as someone who heard it on the road with you. (Thank you, LapitaPolata.)

Developer tools

- New cr.testall tests: reply_language_everywhere, companion_news_fresh.

Notes

- Your existing saves load as they are.
