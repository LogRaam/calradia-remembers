Version 2.5.25

The prisoner envoys from 2.5.24, fixed after the first audiences you reported. Thank you, fkasad.

Fixed

- The envoy no longer "hesitates, and waits for you to speak" turn after turn. His replies had a much smaller budget than ordinary conversation, and a model that thinks before answering (many local models do) spent all of it thinking and said nothing. He now has the same budget as any conversation.

- A captive you give away is a gift. Telling the envoy the prisoner is yours for nothing used to pay you his lord's whole budget anyway. Now no gold changes hands, and his lord remembers it as a gift.

- A captive ransomed by his own side goes home free. He was put in his own lord's cells, and the game then reported him as having escaped. He is now simply ransomed home, and bears you no grudge for it. This also applies to ransoms agreed in ordinary conversation.

- The envoy makes the first offer and haggles. He opens at a lower figure and rises only when pressed, up to what his lord allowed and never beyond it, and gives his last word after a few counter-offers.

- When his lord holds none of your people, the envoy says plainly that an exchange is not on the table.

- An envoy no longer comes while his lord is a prisoner himself, when no deal could be made in the lord's name.

- The envoy's conversation now appears in the log like any other, and cr.prompt_dump during an audience writes the envoy's prompt to a file.

Notes

- Your existing saves load as they are.
