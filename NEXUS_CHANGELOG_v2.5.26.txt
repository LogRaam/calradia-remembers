Version 2.5.26

A small update: a fix for inherited characters, and safer in-game self-tests.

Fixed

- If your character retires (or you switch to another character with a mod), the people who knew your predecessor no longer speak of them as dead. They now know your predecessor stepped down and still lives.

- The succession self-test in cr.testall no longer changes the save you run it on. It used to run a real succession, erasing characters' private memories of you. If you ran cr.testall on a real save before this version, that save may have lost those memories.

New

- cr.testall now checks the prisoner trade from the last two updates on real lords: a captive ransomed by his own family goes home free, a captive given away costs nothing, and an exchange of two captives works both ways. Each test puts everything back as it was.

Notes

- Your existing saves load as they are.
