Version 2.5.26

A small update: a fix for inherited characters, and developer self-test work.

Fixed

- If your character retires (or you switch to another character with a mod), the people who knew your predecessor no longer speak of them as dead. They now know your predecessor stepped down and still lives.

Developer tools

- cr.testall: the succession self-test no longer runs a real succession on the test save, and new self-tests cover the prisoner trade from the last two updates (a ransom home, a gift, an exchange).

Notes

- Your existing saves load as they are.
