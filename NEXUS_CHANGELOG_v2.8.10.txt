Version 2.8.10

Women in the ranks, a button to report a problem from the chat, characters for one campaign, translatable settings, new doors for other mods, and a round of fixes from your reports on Nexus and Discord.

New

- Women in the ranks. In battle, a share of the soldiers of Sturgia, Battania and the Khuzaits (one in four), and of the Nords and the Southern Empire (one in ten), are women, with a woman's face and the armour of their troop. The other cultures stay as they are. Heroes, companions and troops that are already women are never changed. The shares are in ModuleData/women_in_ranks.json, which you can copy into your Custom folder and change; the MCM setting "Women In The Ranks" turns it off.
- Report a problem, from the chat. A button in the chat window opens a window where you write what went wrong and see the whole report as it will be saved: the settings that shape a reply, the conversation, the latest exchanges with the model and this conversation's part of the log. Keys are masked. The report is saved to a file and written into the log, ready to send. Nothing is sent anywhere. The MCM can hide the button.
- Characters for one campaign. A character_overrides.json in Custom/campaigns/<your campaign>/ applies to that campaign only, on top of the shared one, so characters written for one playthrough no longer appear in the next. (Thank you, Bhelogan.)
- The choices in the MCM drop-down lists can now be translated, like the rest of the settings page. Choices you already saved are kept. (Thank you, D.C.Michael.)

Fixed

- On Bannerlord 1.5.7, a hero marked as executed but brought back to life no longer crashes the game on the next day.
- The character who sold you a workshop now remembers the sale and no longer contests that it is yours. (Thank you, Windmolen.)
- A second bandit camp near the same town is now its own task, not refused as a repeat of the first. (Thank you, Aporo.)
- The head of a house no longer asks you to break up your own house for a wedding that two heads of houses cannot make. (Thank you, Aporo.)
- The purse now says that it writes an offer, and that no coin moves until you confirm. A "Learn more" window explains how a payment is made and how to tell a payment done, declined or refused. (Thank you, Aporo.)
- After switching to another character with a player switcher, characters know who you now play. (Thank you, Icezera.)
- cr.reload_prompts and cr.reload_lore now apply to the next reply, without reloading the save. (Thank you, Icezera.)
- The conversation panel no longer shows the markers other mods leave in a character's text, or a secret they marked as one. The character still reads the text whole. (Thank you, Bhelogan.)

For modders

- CrPrompt.Reload() and CrLore.Reload(): the console reloads, as API. (Thank you, Bhelogan.)
- CrDiplomacy: another mod can be the authority over the wars, peaces and changes of realm CR makes, approving or refusing each before it happens. (Thank you, VonLemberg.)
- CrWorld: what a character knows of the world; world events fed by other mods can be secret, known only to the heroes named. (Thank you, VonLemberg.)
- CrPrompt.StockSections and CrPrompt.Sections: the stock text of each prompt section for one character, for a profile editor. (Thank you, Bhelogan.)
- CrPortraits.RegisterSource: a mod can supply a chat portrait per hero. (Thank you, Bhelogan.)
- CrCharacterText: a mod can write text about a character for the prompt and the panel, without editing character_overrides.json. (Thank you, Bhelogan.)
- Feed.List and CrAims.List: a mod reads back the world events and aims it gave CR. (Thank you, Bhelogan.)
- CrApi.Version reports the version of the extension API. EXTENSION_API.md describes every door.

Developer tools

- cr.test all gains campaign_overrides, character_text, diplomacy_door, executionerless_death, feed_secrets, panel_text_clean, player_report, portrait_source, prompt_follows_player, prompt_stock_sections, women_in_ranks and workshop_sale_memory.
- cr.test all now fails a test that leaves a captive in your prison.

Notes

- Saves from 2.8.9 and older load as they are, and your MCM choices are kept.
