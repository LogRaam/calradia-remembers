Version 2.5.4

Your saves from 2.5.3 carry over untouched.

Fixed
- The crash when opening a letter, or the Balance of Power panel, is fixed at its cause. Two players reported the game closing a few seconds after a panel opened, and not every time. The reason was in how the mod opened those panels. Bannerlord opens its own Clan, Kingdom and Inventory screens by changing the game's state, which genuinely suspends the world map. Calradia Remembers was only stacking a picture on top of the map instead, so the map went right on running underneath the panel, invisible but fully alive. Measured in game over four seconds: with the old method the map ticked 481 times under an open panel, exactly as much as with nothing open at all; with the new one, zero. One of the things still running under there was the game's own siege animation, which moves catapult shots through the engine's native code, and that is what brought the game down. It also explains why it only happened sometimes: it needed a siege to be actually bombarding somewhere in Calradia at that moment. Both panels are now opened the way the game opens its own screens.
- Honest note on that fix: neither report could be reproduced here, on any screen resolution or interface scale. So this repairs the mechanism that produced the crash rather than a crash we watched happen. If a panel still misbehaves for you, the mod now writes down, every single time you open one, whether the map really went quiet underneath it, which will say plainly whether this was the cause.
- Two of the mod's internal safety guards can no longer fail silently. If the installed version of the game does not expose something a guard needs, that is now always written to the log, instead of going to a log channel that is switched off by default. The one fact that would have explained the crash above was impossible to obtain from a normal install.

Changed
- Closing the Balance of Power panel now takes you back to the clan screen you opened it from, instead of to the world map. It opens over the clan screen the way any game screen opens over another, rather than closing it first.

Also
- For the players who reported NPCs they cannot start a Calradia Remembers conversation with: the mod now records, once per conversation, whether its chat option was offered at all and which dialogue steps the conversation went through. If that has happened to you, turn logging on in the mod options, reproduce it once, and send the log. It should name the cause without any guesswork.
