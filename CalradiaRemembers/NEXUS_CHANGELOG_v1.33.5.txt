Version 1.33.5

Quality-of-life additions and fixes from player feedback. Your saves carry over untouched.

New
- Lock an NPC's kinks in the character override file. If you author characters in ModuleData/character_overrides.json, you can now add a "kinks" list to lock specific kinks on an NPC (the full list of valid names is in that file). Unlike adding them by console, an authored lock survives a lore reload, shows up when you inspect the NPC, and drives the scenes you stage. Requires "Use custom traits" in the mod options.
- Dark Leather size option. A new "Dark Leather Size (%)" setting in the mod options scales the leather chat window, so you can make it larger on a high-resolution screen where it felt cramped. 100 is the shipped size, and the Origin skin is unaffected.

Improved
- Characters hold truer to their nature. A cold, cruel, or calculating lord could drift into kindly, agreeable answers that did not fit them (a ruthless lord suddenly championing the peasantry). The prompt now presses harder to keep replies in line with the character's own traits.
- NPCs stop putting your army in the room. In a city or a keep, an NPC would speak as though your soldiers stood at your back, when your men would really be beyond the walls. Indoors they now know your force is not present, while still aware of your standing as a commander; out on the open road it is unchanged.
