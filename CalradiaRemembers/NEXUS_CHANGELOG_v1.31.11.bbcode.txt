[size=4]v1.31.11[/size]

A new feature, from a player request: you can now play matchmaker. Saves carry over untouched.

[size=3][b]New[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Arrange a marriage between your kin and another noble's house.[/b] You can now propose a match between one of your own unwed kin and an eligible noble of another clan, a marriage in which you are neither party. Speak with the head of another house (or a noble parent) and, if you have unwed kin of your own, you may put the match to them. They weigh your standing and the gain to their house: a family that thinks well of you agrees, and so does one whose kin would marry up into a greater clan, while a stranger of no greater standing is turned away. The wedding is real and follows the game's own rules of who may wed whom. (Request: CarolusIV on Nexus.)[/*]
[/list]
