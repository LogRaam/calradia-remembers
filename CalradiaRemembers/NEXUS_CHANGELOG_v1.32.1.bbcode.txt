[size=4]v1.32.1[/size]

Two fixes on top of v1.32.0, both from player reports. Saves carry over untouched.

[size=3][b]Fixed[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Compatibility with total-conversion overhauls.[/b] Calradia Remembers no longer declares hard dependencies on two base modules it never actually used (StoryMode, CustomBattle). An overhaul that replaces the vanilla campaign and does not load those (e.g. Empires of Europe 1100) could stop the game from launching at all, with nothing in Crash Doctor (a launcher-level module error, not a code crash); it now loads alongside them. (Report: a player on Nexus.)[/*]
[*][b]A violent or coerced encounter no longer leaves a false "tender moment."[/b] A character's encyclopedia could show a fond kindness ("carries a small kindness remembered towards you for a tender moment we shared") after a hostile scene that actually lowered your relation, because it was recorded on any moment the AI tagged as intimate, with no check on the scene's tone. A fond memory now forms only when the moment neither lowered your relation nor was a captive/coerced scene. A hostile encounter is still remembered, it just no longer leaves a false tenderness on the page. (Report: a player on Nexus.)[/*]
[/list]
