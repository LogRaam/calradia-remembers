[size=4]v1.32.1[/size]

A new look for the conversation, a new thing to do with your prisoners, and compatibility fixes. Saves carry over untouched.

[size=3][b]Added[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]A new chat window: "Dark Leather."[/b] Conversations now open in an illuminated two-page leather book, the new default look. The exchange is on the right page; click any portrait to open a dossier on the left, the speaker's culture, age, role, standing, background, what you have learned about them, and their grievances. Click your OWN portrait to read your own notable deeds. Prefer the original panel? A new [b]Chat Window Style[/b] option in the mod menu switches between [b]Origin[/b] (the classic panel) and [b]Dark Leather[/b] at any time. It is the same conversation either way, only the look differs. (More skins are planned.)[/*]
[*][b]Dress your prisoners.[/b] Give a held prisoner a garment (a gown, a fine tunic) in conversation and she actually puts it on, visible the next time you speak with her. A captor imposes the garment regardless of how the prisoner regards you; only clothing is ever handed over this way, never a weapon.[/*]
[/list]

[size=3][b]Fixed[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Compatibility with total-conversion overhauls.[/b] Calradia Remembers no longer declares hard dependencies on two base modules it never actually used (StoryMode, CustomBattle). An overhaul that replaces the vanilla campaign and does not load those (e.g. Empires of Europe 1100) could stop the game from launching at all, with nothing in Crash Doctor (a launcher-level module error, not a code crash); it now loads alongside them. (Report: a player on Nexus.)[/*]
[*][b]A violent or coerced encounter no longer leaves a false "tender moment."[/b] A character's encyclopedia could show a fond kindness ("a tender moment we shared") after a hostile scene that actually lowered your relation, because it was recorded on any moment the AI tagged as intimate, with no check on the scene's tone. A fond memory now forms only when the moment neither lowered your relation nor was a captive/coerced scene. A hostile encounter is still remembered, it just no longer leaves a false tenderness on the page. (Report: a player on Nexus.)[/*]
[*][b]Compatibility with Immersive Recruit Commoners.[/b] The townsfolk and villager chat option used to sit on the raw conversation opening, where it could crowd out another mod's options (IRC's recruit lines). It now hangs off the normal town/village talk menu, so both mods' options coexist. (Report: a player on Nexus.)[/*]
[*][b]A grouped execution shows a single notice.[/b] Executing several captives at once now raises one banner for the deed instead of one per grieving kinsman.[/*]
[/list]
