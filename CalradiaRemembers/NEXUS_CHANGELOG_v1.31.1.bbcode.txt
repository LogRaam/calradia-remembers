[size=4]v1.31.1[/size]

A same-day follow-up to v1.31.0, from what you reported within hours of it going up. Small, and all four are things you would have run into.

[list]
[*][b]Characters no longer recite the game's plumbing.[/b] Some models answer with a function call, [i]{"name": "change_relation", "parameters": {"delta": X}}[/i], instead of the format the mod teaches. Nothing read it, so the regard never actually moved, and nothing removed it, so the character said it out loud. Both halves are fixed: the intent is now honoured (still capped and rate-limited as always) and the raw text never reaches the conversation.

[*][b]No more endless procession of lords who never speak.[/b] If a meeting's conversation failed to start, the mod waited for it forever, and that wait blocked every later meeting from opening: lord after lord would ride up to you and nothing would happen. The failure is now detected the moment it occurs, and the conversation opens directly instead.

[*][b]Riding alone now means riding alone.[/b] A party of one counts you yourself, and that was being reported to characters as "a handful of companions ride with them", so they spoke, and narrated, as though you had an escort you did not have. Your solitude is now stated plainly instead of left unsaid, and characters are told not to furnish you with riders, guards or servants.

[*][b]Eye colour is described correctly again.[/b] Amber eyes were being called green, and the whole middle of the range was off by a similar margin (greys read as deep blue, the greens ran too late). The table was rebuilt from eighteen measured samples across the full range.
[/list]

Saves carry over untouched, as always.
