[size=4]v1.31.8[/size]

Characters stop forgetting a plan you just made with them, several player-reported fixes, duels and forced marriage arrive, and a new door lets other mods read and write what a character remembers. Saves carry over untouched.

[size=3][b]For everyone[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Characters no longer forget a plan you just made with them.[/b] You could agree on something with a character.. a family member offering to gather information and report back to you that same day.. let a little time pass, return, and find they had no memory of it, sometimes indignant that you would claim such a thing. A conversation's memory used to be kept only when the model happened to flag a dramatic "event"; a plan or a promise it did not flag was lost when the window closed. Now every conversation of substance leaves a memory, the summary is told to keep any plan, promise, or arrangement, and it is written on a durable queue that survives a save, a quit, or a crash. (Report: a player on Nexus.)[/*]
[*][b]A mercenary contract now pays what it should.[/b] Signing on as a mercenary paid a fiftieth of the agreed sum.. a contract worth thousands handed over a few dozen denars. It now pays in full. (Report: a player.)[/*]
[*][b]A bandit nemesis no longer "forgets" who he is mid-captivity.[/b] The named bandit boss who holds you could seem to become a different man partway through, with no memory of what came before.. a save, a reload, or a prisoner transfer could quietly mint a second boss who inherited the first one's memories. He is now anchored to one identity, restored intact across saves and never silently replaced. (Report: a player.)[/*]
[*][b]A fond memory is no longer duplicated.[/b] A character's encyclopedia could show the same tender moment three separate times. Repeats of the same moment are now counted ("three times over") rather than stacked as identical copies, which also stops them crowding out everything else the character remembers of you. Old saves are repaired automatically. (Report: a player screenshot.)[/*]
[*][b]A meeting is no longer called off while you ride toward it.[/b] Riding straight at an announced lord could cancel the meeting out from under you. As long as you are actually closing the gap, it now waits for you. (Report: a player.)[/*]
[*][b]File logging actually writes now.[/b] Turning logging on could produce no file and no explanation, because the game does not create the Logs folder the mod writes into. The folder is created now, and if a log still cannot be written you are told once, on screen, with the path it tried. (Report: a player.)[/*]
[/list]

[size=3][b]New[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Duels, from a conversation.[/b] You can challenge a character to a duel in chat, and a character may challenge you; refusing a challenge is a real, remembered act, and it cannot be farmed. Duels can be fought in the wilderness or at a town's gates.[/*]
[*][b]A family can marry a daughter against a lukewarm bond.[/b] When a match is blessed by the family but your personal standing with the bride falls short, the marriage now goes ahead (the family's will prevails) and the bride carries a real, mendable resentment scaled to how far it fell short, instead of the match quietly failing for no visible reason.[/*]
[*][b]Captive scenes read less repetitively.[/b] Each scene is now written through a rotating lens, so the prose no longer leans on the same handful of phrasings from one scene to the next.[/*]
[/list]

[size=3][b]For modders[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]A new door onto a character's own memory, and onto conversations.[/b] Other mods can read what a character remembers, inject memories it will treat as its own, and listen for a conversation opening (with the character id, the save id, and the in-game day) or closing (with the whole attributed transcript, witness lines included). It joins the existing world-event and mission doors. See the "For modders" section on this page.[/*]
[/list]
