A character keeps their own voice at the council table. Your saves from 2.2.2 carry over untouched.

Fixed
- A character keeps their own manner at a council. A courteous, composed, knight-like character could turn unexpectedly blunt or rough once seated at a council, while the others stayed themselves. The council was describing each member only by their culture and rank, dropping the individual character that carries their voice, so a polite lord of a warlike people fell back to a rough, warrior tone at the table. Each member now carries their own nature into the council and keeps the same manner and level of politeness they use in a private word, so their voice stays consistent between a one-to-one talk and a meeting.
