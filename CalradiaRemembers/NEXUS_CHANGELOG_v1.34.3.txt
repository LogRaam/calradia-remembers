Version 1.34.3

Three fixes from player feedback. Your saves carry over untouched.

New
- Recruit a captured lord to your side. Persuade a hero prisoner you hold, once you have earned enough standing with them, to switch sides: they are freed, swear into your clan, and join your party. Before, a captured noble could be talked into joining but nothing actually happened.

Fixed
- Your narrative style is remembered. The writing style you pick is now saved and restored on the next launch, and survives a mod update, instead of resetting to Default on every restart.
- Asking a lord to ride out with you is more reliable. When a lord agrees to follow you on the map, the model is now pushed to actually set the escort in motion rather than only describing it in prose (a weaker model used to just narrate riding out and nothing followed); and it will no longer pretend you rode out together when the lord has no field party to bring along.
