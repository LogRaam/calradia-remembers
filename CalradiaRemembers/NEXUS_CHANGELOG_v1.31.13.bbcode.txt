[size=4]v1.31.13[/size]

Two features: choose the voice your story is told in, and a memory limit so long-lived saves stay quick. Saves carry over untouched.

[size=3][b]New[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Choose the voice the story is told in.[/b] A new quill button in the chat window opens a picker of writing styles that change how the prose READS, never who a character is or what they will do. Fifteen ship with the mod, from plain Novelistic, Terse, and Grimdark to author-anchored voices (Tolkien, Martin, Cornwell, Austen, McCarthy, Dumas, Kay, Homer, Dostoevsky, Howard, and more). Your choice applies everywhere, to lords and commoners alike, and to whichever model you run. Styles are plain text files under ModuleData/Styles: edit one or drop in your own, even in the middle of a game, and your next line uses it. (Console: cr.styles, cr.style.)[/*]
[*][b]A limit on how much a character remembers, so veteran saves stay quick.[/b] As a character piles up remembered moments, the mod now folds the oldest into a short background outline instead of handing the AI a character's entire history on every message. Long-lived saves keep their speed and cost in check without losing the shape of a character's past. On by default, with a toggle in the mod options. (From a modder's report of a conversation's context running past 20,000 tokens.)[/*]
[/list]
