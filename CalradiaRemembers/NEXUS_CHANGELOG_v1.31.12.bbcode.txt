A small consistency fix, from a player report. Saves carry over untouched.

[size=3][b]Fixed[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]A character's memories now keep one voice.[/b] Some remembered moments were written in the first person ("I told him...") and others in the third ("Nethor granted...", "he admits..."), so a character's memory panel could read unevenly. New memories are now always written in the character's own first-person voice, matching the conversation recaps. Memories already saved keep whatever voice they were written in (the mod does not rewrite them). (Report: a player on Nexus.)[/*]
[/list]
