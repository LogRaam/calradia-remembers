Version 2.1.4

A model switch in the chat, actions you can take by hand, and a redesigned Memories page. Your saves from 2.1.3 carry over untouched.

New
- A model switch in the chat window. It cycles between Enhanced (two models for the best result, a writer plus an action reader), Roleplay (one lighter model), and NSFW (the explicit model). Roleplay and NSFW are a single call, lighter on local setups.
- An Actions button. Emit an action by hand (give gold, free a prisoner, appoint a governor, and so on) when you want to be explicit, or when the character missed what you meant.
- A redesigned Memories page, on illuminated parchment. Each entry that carried a real deed (coin changing hands, regard rising or falling, someone recruited, a fief granted) is marked in blue, so the turning points in your history with a character stand out.
- A tavern keeper may point you to a nemesis you have already met who ranges nearby, naming him and his region.

Fixed
- Characters who ask for a word inside a settlement now show their portrait, open the conversation knowing it was they who sought you out, and no longer send a "lost track of you" notice hours after you have already spoken with them.
- A noble or notable standing in a town you hold now knows you are its lord, instead of asking what brings you to their city.
- Giving a character a large sum of gold now registers the full amount.

For mod authors
- A new voicing surface: an addon can subscribe to each line as it is spoken (speaker, text, and voice hints) to add text-to-speech, subtitles, or lip-sync. A how-to ships in the module's Extension folder.
