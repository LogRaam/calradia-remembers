Version 2.4.3

Characters answer with what they actually know, in their own words. Your saves carry over untouched.

Fixed
- Ask a character about a specific lord or war and you get an answer grounded in what they know, not invention. Until now a character only offered the few rumours highest on their mind, chosen without regard to your question, so asking about something specific could draw a made-up reply. Now, alongside that ambient news, a character also raises what they genuinely know that bears on what you just said (matched to the people and subjects you mention), and only what they could plausibly have heard.
- Characters relay news in their own voice instead of word for word. A piece of news phrased as a tidy sentence could be recited verbatim by some models; a character now treats it as something they know and tells it in their own words.
