Version 1.33.6

More fixes and a safeguard, all from your feedback. Your saves carry over untouched.

New
- Keep your custom prompts and styles safe from updates. A new mod option, "Custom Folder Path", lets you point the mod at a folder OUTSIDE the mod for your edited prompts and styles, so a reinstall or a clean update can never wipe them (leave it blank for the default ModuleData/Custom). It also lets you keep several prompt sets and switch between them.

Improved
- A promised task now actually becomes a quest. When a character agrees in conversation to send you on a concrete mission or errand, the quest is created that same turn, instead of the agreement sometimes evaporating into back-and-forth talk with nothing recorded.

Fixed
- Captured at sea no longer plays out in a land camp. If you are taken prisoner at sea (for instance by Sea Raiders), the scene now knows you are aboard a ship rather than inventing a tent by a campfire.
- Coercion is no longer remembered as intimacy. A non-consensual captive scene is never recorded as a consensual intimate memory, and never warms a character's regard as though it were one; the harm is remembered as what it is.
