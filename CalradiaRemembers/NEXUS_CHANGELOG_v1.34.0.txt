Version 1.34.0

Characters can now act on what you agree to in a one-to-one conversation, plus a new cross-talk option and a pregnancy fix. Your saves carry over untouched.

New
- Characters carry out commands and appointments right in conversation. When it genuinely fits your situation, a character may act on what you agree to, one to one, instead of only at a council: name a companion or vassal as governor of one of your fiefs, give a companion a party role (scout, engineer, quartermaster, surgeon), call a companion back into your party, send a companion off on an errand (bring back word of the realm, spy, and so on), set a standing stipend, or take a sworn oath; and as a ruler, grant or revoke a vassal's fief, or dismiss a companion from your clan. Each is only offered when it truly applies, and the game checks it again before anything actually happens.
- New option: NPC Cross-Talk. Controls how many times the NPCs in a scene may bounce a remark off one another before the turn comes back to you (0 to 3, default 2; set it to 0 so only the character you spoke to replies).

Fixed
- Pregnancies no longer last forever on a no-births campaign. If your campaign has the Life and Death cycle turned off, a pregnancy could never be delivered and a character would keep bringing it up with no end; such a pregnancy now quietly ends on its own, and none is started while births are disabled.
