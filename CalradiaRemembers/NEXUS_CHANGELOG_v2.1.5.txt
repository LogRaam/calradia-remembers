Version 2.1.5

A companion's vow is theirs to keep, not a quest you can fail. Your saves from 2.1.4 carry over untouched.

Fixed
- A character's pledge is no longer treated as your quest. When a companion or lord vowed something to you (to fight at your side, to pay you a sum, to keep a peace), the mod wrongly turned it into an entry in your own quest journal that could close as failed, and then cost you their regard and planted a grudge, even when there was never a chance to honor it. A companion who governs a fief, for example, can never ride into battle beside you. A vow is the character's own word, not a task for you. It is now simply remembered: it appears in their Memories, has no deadline, opens no quest, and never changes your relationship on its own. Old pledges carried over from an earlier save are quietly retired with no penalty.
