Version 2.2.1

Your letters become a book you can leaf through. Your saves from 2.2.0 carry over untouched.

New
- Your letters are now an open book. Reading a letter opens a two-page leather book: the whole correspondence with that person runs down the left page (every letter that has ever passed between you, kept for good, oldest to newest), and the letter in your hand sits on the right. Their portrait rides the page, using a custom chat portrait if you have dropped one in, otherwise their usual face.
- Intercepted letters can go astray (off by default). Turn on "Intercepted letters can leak" in the mod settings and a letter waylaid on the road may fall into the wrong hands, and someone who wishes you ill may write to you about what they read.
- A quieter or a busier room. A new "Witness Density" setting lets you thin the crowd that gathers around a conversation, or leave it as full as it is now.
- Council decisions that stick. The accords your council reaches are now remembered as standing agreements: a character brings them up when you speak one to one, and you can review them on their encyclopedia page. A hostage pledged to you whose house then breaks faith is forfeit to you and becomes your prisoner.

Fixed
- Backing out of a reply no longer closes the letter. When you started a reply and changed your mind, Cancel shut the whole window. It now simply returns you to reading the letter, so you can still acknowledge it and leave the ordinary way.
- A slow letter is given more time. Writing a letter waited only thirty seconds on the model before giving up; it now waits sixty, and a slow reply is no longer reported to you as an error.
