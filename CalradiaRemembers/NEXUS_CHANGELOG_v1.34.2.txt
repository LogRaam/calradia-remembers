Version 1.34.2

A resilience release. The conversation actions are now more robust and fairer when the world changes between what is said and what is done. No new features; your saves carry over untouched.

Fixed and hardened
- Actions re-check the world at the moment they happen. Naming a governor now respects an already-filled post and a companion who is away on an errand; joining a kingdom you are at war with is refused; revoking a fief releases its sitting governor.
- You are no longer blamed for what the world does. An oath to keep the peace is not broken when someone attacks YOU (only the one who marches breaks it); a promisor taken captive is not branded an oathbreaker while he can do nothing; a quest whose target dies, or a pledge whose rival dies, is quietly voided instead of penalizing you.
- Prisoner and gold dealings are safe and honest. No captive is handed over or sold without a real holder to receive them; gold moves only after the trade is certain, and is refunded if it fails; a lord cannot buy a captive for a pittance while claiming the full price; and a member of your own house is never treated as merchandise.
- Captive-scene actions respect their own rules again (the darker ones only apply while you are genuinely a prisoner in a Hardcore scene).
- Companions are looked after. A stipend refunds the remainder and tells you if its recipient dies; a companion away on an errand can no longer wrongly quit or stay away forever; an expelled companion will not reappear in your party.
- Marriage and persuasion are fairer. Arranging a match now respects captivity and war; a divorce blocked only because your spouse was handed a role now proceeds instead of falsely closing ranks against you; swaying a lord no longer defaults to the harmful direction on a garbled request.
- The chat, the story log, and the quest journal now tell the same truth as the game: no logged sum the purse never saw, a refused action leaves a clear note, and an unverified quest-complete claim tells you plainly that nothing was paid.
- Under the hood: unreachable dead code removed, and a new quiet diagnostic watches for actions the AI proposes out of context (log only, for future tuning).
