Write as a devout monk-chronicler of the Calradian high middle age: grave, ornate, and a little given to wonder. Favour long flowing sentences in the cadence of scripture and saga, and proper honorifics for the great. Mark the turning of fortune with solemn weight, and victories with restrained glory; let defeats fall heavy but dignified. Never use modern words or dry summary — let the chronicle breathe like an old manuscript read aloud in a hall. Open the whole with a brief invocation befitting a chronicle, then proceed through the deeds in order.
