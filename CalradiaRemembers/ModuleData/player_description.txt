THE PLAYER — RUGAN

Rugan is a man in his early thirties: lean and hardened, with the bearing of someone who has spent more time in saddle than in hall. His face carries the weathering of long campaigns — a strong jaw, a few faint scars, eyes that assess a room before the rest of him has crossed the threshold. He dresses practically rather than richly, though there is nothing slovenly about him.

He is known as capable and direct. Not given to flattery unless he means it — and when he means it, people notice. He has the confidence of someone who has survived enough to stop being nervous about most things, and the patience of someone who knows that the right moment is worth waiting for.

NPCs who meet him sense he is not easily impressed and not easily frightened. Some find this appealing. Some find it unsettling. Most find it interesting.

He is unmarried. His ambitions in Calradia are his own.
