Version 2.1.3

Choose who is in the room when you confront a prisoner. Your saves from 2.1.2 carry over untouched.

New
- Any prisoner you hold can be confronted, not only your nemesis. Talking to a captured lord from the party screen now lets you open a private confrontation with them, with its own captor's line. Before, only a tracked nemesis could be brought to that scene.
- Bring a companion into the confrontation. You can hand the prisoner to one of your own companions, who will then act on their own initiative rather than only stand and watch, in their own voice and manner.
- Bring another of your prisoners as leverage. A second captive you hold can be brought in, coerced and in chains, so you can use one against the other. They are present under duress, never a free voice in the room and never a threat to you.

These are part of the mod's mature captor and captive scenes. The explicit layer follows your Adult Content setting, and none of it appears at the lowest setting.
