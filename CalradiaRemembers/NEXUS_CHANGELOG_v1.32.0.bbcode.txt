[size=4]v1.32.0[/size]

A new look for the conversation, a new thing to do with your prisoners, and a compatibility fix. Saves carry over untouched.

[size=3][b]Added[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]A new chat window: "Dark Leather."[/b] Conversations now open in an illuminated two-page leather book, the new default look. The exchange is on the right page; click any portrait to open a dossier on the left, the speaker's culture, age, role, standing, background, what you have learned about them, and their grievances. Click your OWN portrait to read your own notable deeds. Prefer the original panel? A new [b]Chat Window Style[/b] option in the mod menu switches between [b]Origin[/b] (the classic panel) and [b]Dark Leather[/b] at any time. It is the same conversation either way, only the look differs. (More skins are planned.)[/*]
[*][b]Dress your prisoners.[/b] Give a held prisoner a garment (a gown, a fine tunic) in conversation and she actually puts it on, visible the next time you speak with her. A captor imposes the garment regardless of how the prisoner regards you; only clothing is ever handed over this way, never a weapon.[/*]
[/list]

[size=3][b]Fixed[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Compatibility with Immersive Recruit Commoners.[/b] Calradia Remembers' townsfolk and villager chat option used to sit on the raw conversation opening, where it could crowd out another mod's options (IRC's recruit lines). It now hangs off the normal town/village talk menu, so both mods' options coexist. (Report: a player on Nexus.)[/*]
[*][b]A grouped execution shows a single notice.[/b] Executing several captives at once now raises one banner for the deed instead of one per grieving kinsman.[/*]
[/list]
