Version 2.6.8

Characters with a mood of their own and a regard for themselves, several fixes from your reports, and one rule for everything a character does.

Fixed

- Addressing a witness by name in Japanese now hands them the turn. The name at the start of your line followed by 、 (or ！/？), or at the end after 、, counts as speaking to them. (Thank you, yozakura12.)
- A lord announced as riding out to meet you now actually comes, instead of passing you by or riding into a town. If he is called into an army on the way, he stands down. (Thank you, Lordfadooboo.)
- A dead family member no longer takes a seat at your council. (Thank you, Maifailun.)
- Everything a character does now passes the same checks first: never the dead, never someone the game has taken out of the world, and never a child or a captive where that would make no sense. This closes several small holes: letters received by characters no longer in the world, arrest warrants in the name of a dead or captive ruler, and a few more.

New

- A friendly clan warms a first meeting. When a clan already counts you a friend, its members start warmer toward you, once, up to friendly regard; the rest is still earned in person. Someone you have offended keeps it. Option in MCM, on by default. (Thank you, tashmetu.)
- The mood of the day. Each character has a day: wounded, short of money, their realm winning or losing a war, at ease in a hall or out in the field. It colours how they speak, never what they agree to. You can lift or sour it during a conversation, and a letter carries the mood of the day it was written.
- Self-esteem. Characters you have met now have a regard for themselves. It starts from their station, their own nature and their valour, and it moves with what happens to them: battles, tournaments, fiefs won and lost, a death in the family, a duel with you, and captivity, where each time weighs more than the last. It mends slowly. A shaken lord is quicker to take offence and wants reassurance; a proud one expects deference. (Thank you, Lordfadooboo and Maifailun.)
- How they seem. The character panel in the chat has a new line: one sentence on how they carry themselves and how their day shows. A proud or calculating lord hides a shaken spirit from strangers; if you know them well, you will see it.

For modders

- RegardChanged has a new cause, clan_standing. It comes from the game's own relation, so a mod that mirrors regard into that relation should skip it. EXTENSION_API.md now says AddMemory is called on the game thread, and that an injected memory never moves regard. (Thank you, tashmetu.)

Developer tools

- New cr.testall tests: interception_holds_course, clan_standing_seed, mood_of_the_day, self_esteem. initiative_gate is now action_gate; council_attendance checks the dead.

Notes

- Your existing saves load as they are.
