Version 2.5.8

Your saves from 2.5.7 carry over untouched. Everything below came from a report on this page or the bug tracker, and most of it is the same lesson wearing different clothes: when the mod says nothing about something, the AI does not read that as "forbidden" or "unknown". It reads it as "nobody mentioned it", and fills the gap itself.

Fixed

- Duels are fought again instead of talked about. FloatingMammal: "none of the lords I 'challenged' to a duel went anywhere other than a dialogue driven event... I think now they're assuming its a sort spar, even when the wording 'duel' is being said by me continuously... I just want to beat up some lords in single combat." He was right, and the reason was one line. A duel needs somewhere to fight, and the check asked whether you were in a TOWN. A castle was refused, and a castle is where you meet lords more than anywhere else. The AI was never told the duel was impossible, only never offered it, so it improvised a sparring scene and you were never told why. The mod now asks the game whether the settlement has a walkable courtyard at all, so a castle counts, and a kind of settlement added by a future patch or another mod counts without anyone remembering to add it.

- And you can call a lord out yourself. "Challenge to a duel" now appears in the manual action menu, so wanting to fight someone no longer depends on the AI noticing that you said so. It runs down the same road an AI-initiated challenge takes: the same acceptance rules, the same weight given to the gap in your stations, the same witnesses. The lord can still refuse you. This opens the door; it does not walk you through it.

- Lords follow you when they agree to. A player: "It feels like the AI using this like never follows me despite it agreeing to :( or send troops to X location.. it never does :(" Escorting was refused whenever the lord's realm was at war with ANYONE. That guard was added so a lord would stay free to answer his liege's muster, which is sound, but in an ordinary campaign nearly every kingdom is at war with somebody nearly always, so the offer was almost never on the table. It is on the table again.

- And a lord who might be called away now warns you as he agrees, not afterwards. If his realm is at war, he tells you plainly that the muster may call him and that he will have to break off when it does. Said in advance, that is a lord being honest with you. Said only afterwards, it is one more promise you watched evaporate, which is the complaint above.

- Characters admit what they cannot know, and what they cannot do. plagueAdvocate: "characters don't really know what happens in game a lot of the time. I asked about a recent battle and they have the enemy numbers and type completely wrong. I also ask them about some goods acquisition... and they made up a whole new story about us raiding caravans (we never did). They also don't seem to know what we have in the inventory." A character is now told the boundary of what reaches them: they do not see your baggage or your purse, they do not know the count and make of an army in a battle they were not at, and there are deeds, like sending troops to a place on the map, that they simply cannot perform however sincerely they might want to. "How would I know that?" is now a good answer for them to give. What has NOT changed is that they may still wonder, guess and repeat a rumour: the point is that a guess now sounds like a guess.

- Compact Prompt mode is finally compact. DuskSymphony, with his own server's refusal attached: "Compact Prompt mode is still sending a prompt with context over 9k" against an 8192-token limit, on a fresh install with the first NPC he spoke to. The list of actions a character can take was going out in full in every single prompt, Compact included: 69 of them, unabridged, about 11,500 characters on their own. A Compact prompt has gone from roughly 22,500 characters to 15,150, which is about 3,800 tokens. If you run a local model with a small context window, this is the release that stops shutting you out.

- Crash when a companion asked for a word at sea. thismfman: "My companion wanted a word at sea and it kept open the window to choose not now or have a word while simultaneously opening the chat window. When we had our conversation, i ended it, and it caused a crash." The safety check asks ten questions before a companion may ask for a private word on the road. Not one of them was true on open water, so the offer sailed through every guard and staged a conversation on the naval map. Nobody asks for a word at sea any more; they wait until you make land.

- Characters remember in the language you are playing in. Reported by a player running a total conversion whose lords and holdings carry German names: "CR starts generating memory in german... Most probably because german names are piling up in the NPC memory. Still, the letter is ok, in english." That last sentence is what solved it. With no Reply Language chosen in the mod options, the mod asked the AI to write each memory "in the same language as the letter below", which invites it to weigh the whole passage, names and all. Where every lord, house and holding is named in German, the names outvote the prose and a perfectly English letter is remembered in German. The conversation itself never drifted, because it has always judged the language from one thing only: what you yourself last wrote. Two further places had never been told anything about language at all, and both feed text back to the AI later: the one that folds older memories into a short background carried into every conversation with that character, and the running recap of a long conversation. All of them now judge from whole sentences, and are told outright that the names of people, places, titles and realms prove nothing about what language a passage is in.

- The console help stops being a list somebody has to remember to update. All 259 commands are now documented, and the mod checks its own completeness, so a command added later cannot go missing from the help in silence. Typing part of a name also finds the family it belongs to: cr.help duel used to explain cr.duel and never mention that duel_challenge, duel_arm, duel_town, duel_courtship and duel_here exist.

Notes

- The Regard Warmth Cooldown option accepts 0 again, and 0 now does what the v2.5.1 changelog promised: it turns the cooldown off, so warmth is worth the same on your hundredth conversation as on your first. Three things had gone wrong at once. The changelog said 0 turned it off, the slider would not let you pick anything below 1, and in the code 0 did the OPPOSITE, freezing the resistance where it stood so affection became permanently DEARER. Only the slider's refusal kept anyone from being bitten by it. Reported by FloatingMammal: "I was wondering if it was intentional to have the affection gain CD be at least a day?" It is not unlimited even at 0: one warm exchange still earns at most one point. The default of 7 is untouched, so nothing changes for anyone who never opens the menu.

- Memories already written in the wrong language stay as they are. Only new ones follow the corrected rule. If you have set a Reply Language in the mod options, none of the language problem ever applied to you.

- Under the hood, a large amount of the mod's decision code was reorganised in this release so that the facts a rule weighs are passed by NAME rather than by position. It is meant to change nothing you can see, and the full test suite plus the in-game self-tests agree that it does not. It is mentioned here only so that, if you do notice something behaving oddly that used to be fine, you know there was a big internal change to point at, and reporting it is worth your time.
