Version 2.6.4

The rest of the ransom fix, and two requests from a modder.

Fixed

- When the game refuses something a character tried to do (a payment from an empty purse, for example), the character no longer invents a story to explain it. Before, a lord whose second payment was refused concluded that you had seized her treasury, and remembered it. The reason is now clearly a rule of the game, not an event in the world.
- A few refusal notices that read like little scenes are now plain statements.
- A deal that moves money by itself (buying, selling or exchanging a captive, paying for silence, paying blackmail, a robbery) is now paid once, even if the character also mentions paying.
- When you buy a captive, the lord knows the price range before naming a figure, and if you pay more or less than was asked, you are told why.

For modders

- A character's culture block in the behavior guidelines is now chosen by their own people. Before, the realm they live in could pull in another people's block: a companion of a player serving a realm named after another culture, or a notable in a town held by another people. (Thank you, tashmetu.)
- cr_patch has a new directive, !block, to replace, add or remove one "If you are from ..." block by its heading, instead of removing its lines one by one. cr_patch and its directives are now documented in HOWTO_Actions, section 7.

Notes

- Your existing saves load as they are.
