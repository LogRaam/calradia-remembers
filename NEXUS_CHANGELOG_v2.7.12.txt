Version 2.7.12

Duty and kin: a liege can hold you to his wars, a family knows its dead, and companions remember every fight. (2.7.11 was skipped: a Nexus upload glitch gave 2.7.10 both numbers.)

New

- A liege can hold his vassal to the war (experimental, off by default: Mod Options, Relationships, "Vassal war duty"). A sworn vassal who has not fought the realm's enemies for 30 days while the realm is at war is summoned by letter to come and answer for it. If you walk into the town or castle where your ruler stands, he calls you to his hall himself; he never rides out after you. Before him, he asks a levy you can pay in any mix of coin, horses, weapons and soldiers. Riding to war settles it at any step. Ignore it, and after 20 more days he takes one of your fiefs (a castle before a town, the poorest), or remembers it if you have none. The window and the size of the levy are set in the options. (Thank you, Maifailun.)
- The manual action menu has a new Intimacy section: "You lay together (a child may come of it)", to record a scene the conversation carried to completion when the interpreter missed it. It is shown only for a spouse, consort or lover, and only at Explicit content or above. The usual roll still decides, and the line does not say whether a child was conceived. (Thank you, tashmetu.)
- On a local model (OpenAI-Compatible), summaries, memory work and letters now wait for the open conversation to close before they are sent, so they no longer slip in between two replies and make them time out. Cloud providers are unchanged. (Thank you, fkasad.)

Fixed

- A mercenary contract signed in the chat no longer ends at once with Mercenary Management, where it was then judged an abandonment. It now has no fixed term and the game's own pay rate, as when you hire in the vanilla dialogue. (Thank you, tashmetu.)
- A lord above you no longer greets you as a servant ("I am at your disposal", "What can I do for you?" and the like). Those lines are kept for a spouse, your own house and close kin, a romance, a dear friend, or when you rule a realm yourself. (Thank you, tashmetu.)
- A parent now knows a child who died, a widow the husband she lost, and a sibling a dead brother or sister, with how and when. Your spouse's family knows if she died, and your other partners know it too. (Thank you, Maifailun and tashmetu.)
- Companions remember the small fights they shared with you (bandits, patrols, a lord's party), folded into one running line so the memory does not fill up with looter bands. A companion hired through the game's own dialogue, and never spoken to in Calradia Remembers, now remembers battles too. (Thank you, Thoragoros1.)
- No audience is offered at a gate you cannot walk through openly: sneaking into a town, or in disguise, no lord or companion calls you aside.
- Giving more than 10,000 at once now says it is "the most that can change hands at once", instead of silently giving less.
- The help for "Adult Content Level" now says a child can be conceived only at Explicit or above, and the log says why a night brought no child (a [PREGNANCY] line). (Thank you, tashmetu.)

Changed

- Mod Options: "Actions Interpreter Timing" is removed. Its "Asynchronous" choice never worked differently; actions are applied with the reply, as they always were. (Thank you, fkasad.)

For modders

- CrMatches: another mod can hand the player a match to broker (suitor, bride, and the one who decides). The decider is then taught to arrange it in the chat, and Calradia Remembers' own rules settle it; CrMatches.Settled reports each answer. Register again when a game is loaded. EXTENSION_API.md, "Matches the player brokers". (Thank you, tashmetu.)
- Written tales: a stage can run consequences as it is entered (on_enter), so a character can leave midway and be given back later in the same tale. New CrTales events: StageReached, Ended and ConsequenceSettled, with each consequence's real outcome. (Thank you, tashmetu.)

Developer tools

- New cr.testall tests: war_duty_ledger, war_duty_reckoning, war_duty_levy, war_duty_audience, aims_conversation, greeting_rank, manual_conception_entry, registered_match, mercenary_join_term, dead_kin, companion_skirmish_memory, background_hold, tale_on_enter.
- cr.war_duty shows the vassal war duty ledger.

Notes
