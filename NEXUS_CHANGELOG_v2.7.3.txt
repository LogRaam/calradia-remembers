Version 2.7.3

Written tales: the black screen fixed for good, and scenes that feel like where you are. Tales are still experimental and OFF by default (MCM, Content, "Authored Quests (experimental)").

Fixed

- Closing a tale's scene left a black screen with no way back. The 2.7.1 fix could never run: the close queued the removal of the dark backdrop for the next moment, then wiped that queue on its way out. It now survives the close, and so do the scene's closing message and a scene that follows a conversation. (Thank you, fkasad, whose log showed exactly where it stuck.)
- The scene's narrator no longer herds you toward what is to be found ("the dark wood to your left", four times). It follows what you do, and mentions a place once. (Thank you, fkasad.)

Written tales

- A scene now knows where it happens and who walks into it: the ground under you (forest, sand, snow, steppe...), the season, the hour, and your character's people. An Aserai in a forest at night and a Battanian at noon no longer read the same wood. (Thank you, fkasad.)
- A tale's last fight can grow with your force, so a lord with an army still gets a climax: the Wolf of the Wood's slavers now muster about six in ten of your healthy men, never fewer than before, at most 120. (Thank you, fkasad.)
- Your prisoners can be invited into a tale's scene only when the tale allows it. Companions and lords riding with you always can, and in ordinary conversations prisoners can still be invited. (Thank you, fkasad.)

Developer tools

- New cr.testall test scene_close_gives_the_map_back: opens a real scene, closes it the way the button does, and checks the map comes back. It writes one [SCENE] error line to the log on purpose.
- New cr.testall test scene_surroundings_read.

For modders

- Two new tale fields: "scale_with_player" on a band, and "invite_prisoners" on a scene (off by default). The Tale Editor has both, and HOWTO_Quests.md documents them.

Notes

- Your existing saves load as they are, a tale already under way included.
