Version 2.5.2

Your saves from 2.5.1 carry over untouched.

New
- Tournaments are remembered, and so are the people you fought. When you enter the lists, the lords who fought there remember how it actually went between you: who you beat, who put you out and in which round, and who took the crown. Afterwards one of them may seek you out about it, or write to you, and how they speak of it follows their character. A proud lord with little honour crows about besting you; an honourable one congratulates you, or is magnanimous when he wins; a hard-fought bout is simply acknowledged with respect. None of it is invented: they speak only of matches that truly happened, as the bracket actually played out. (Suggested by a player.)
- Tournament rivals. Face the same lord in the lists often enough and a standing rivalry forms, warm or sour depending on who he is. An honourable competitor becomes a worthy rival who thinks the better of you for the contest; one with little honour carries the sand of the arena as a grudge instead. Either way, the rivalry is real: it colours how he treats you afterwards, and he has something to say the next time he finds you.

Fixed
- An authored character in character_overrides.json is never passed over in silence again. A character whose only entry was a misspelled orientation was treated as an empty entry and skipped, the same way a character with no "kinks" entry used to be. Now anything you actually wrote counts as authored content, and when a value cannot be understood (a misspelled orientation, a mistyped kink name) the mod tells you instead of ignoring it quietly: cr.lore_status lists the problem under that character, and cr.reload_lore reports both unreadable values and any entry it had nothing to apply from. A typo still never crashes anything; it is simply no longer invisible.
