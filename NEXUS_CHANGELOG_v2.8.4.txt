Version 2.8.4

Folk tales for every people, prisoner sales that follow the conversation, gifts that keep their quality, and lords who no longer flatter your companions.

New

- Folk tales for every people: the Empire, the Aserai, the Sturgians, the Vlandians, the Khuzaits and the Nords, Vakken and Darshi of War Sails now have tales of their own told around the fire, beside the Battanians'. Five tales each, drawn from the game's own lore.

Fixed

- Prisoner sales follow the conversation. The part of the mod that reads what was agreed now sees the captives you hold, your last line and the character's reply before, so "I'll take him at the same price" is understood. A price that names a captive with their house or title is found, a name two captives could answer to sells neither instead of the wrong one, up to eight captives have a price (three with Compact prompt for smaller models), and a sale just made is never undone by being named again the same day. (Thank you, RaveN017Mod.)
- A gift keeps its quality. The bag now lists a Masterwork or Legendary piece apart, with its true name and worth, the very piece you pick is the one that leaves, and the one who receives it wears it with its quality. Sales and goods handed over for a task or a bargain are weighed at that worth too. (Thank you, avalanche24.)
- A lord who barely knows your companion is now told he owes them nothing, and a cruel or tight-fisted one has little patience for forward words from those beneath him, so your companions are no longer always agreed with and praised. (Thank you, froggyluv.)
- Asking a lord to bring his house into your realm no longer ends in a refusal about who rules his kingdom. The chat cannot do that yet: when you rule a kingdom, the notice now says so and points to the game's own conversation with that lord, which can. (Thank you, SgtAlexGreen.)

Developer tools

- cr.test all gains captive_trade_context (it takes a lord captive for the test when you hold none, and frees him after) and gift_keeps_quality.
- cr.barter_spike is an experiment with the game's barter screen for prisoner sales. It moves nothing.

Notes

- Existing saves load as they are.
