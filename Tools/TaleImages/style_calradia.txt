STYLE (applies to every image of a Calradia Remembers tale; the scene itself follows after this block):

World: Calradia, the world of Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord. Its look blends LATE ANTIQUITY and the EARLY MIDDLE AGES,
roughly the 6th to 11th centuries: a fading eastern Roman empire among younger kingdoms. Not the high or late Middle Ages,
not fantasy.

Its peoples, each modelled on a real culture of that age:
- The Empire: late Roman / Byzantine. Lamellar and scale armour, mail, plumed or ridged helmets, red and purple cloth,
  stone and brick, columns, frescoes, mosaics.
- Vlandia: Norman and Frankish. Mail hauberks, kite shields, nasal helmets, early motte-and-bailey keeps, timber and stone.
- Sturgia: Kievan Rus' and Norse. Fur, felt, spangenhelms, round shields, log houses, birch forests, cold rivers.
- Battania: Celtic and Pictish highlanders. Wool, tartan-like cloth, torcs, painted wood, hill forts, misty glens and oaks.
- The Aserai: early Arab and Bedouin. Robes, turbans, light mail, sandstone towns, desert, palms, tents.
- The Khuzait: steppe nomads, Mongol-like. Felt yurts, lamellar, horse bows, endless grassland.
- The Nords: Scandinavian seafarers. Longships, longhouses, fjords, wool and fur.

Never show: real-world religious symbols (no Christian crosses, crescents, stars of David; Calradia has its own gods,
shown only as plain carved posts, standing stones or abstract sun and animal motifs), plate armour, gothic cathedrals
or pointed gothic arches, glazed or paned windows (shutters, oiled hide or open slits instead), gunpowder, printed books, glass windows in a
village, Renaissance or modern clothing, fantasy creatures, magic, elves, dragons, glowing effects, readable text or
lettering, logos, watermarks, signatures, frames or borders.

Look: a painterly, realistic illustration, like a historical painting or a high-quality game concept art: natural
light, muted earthy palette, weathered and lived-in materials, believable people of ordinary build. Grounded and
serious, never cartoonish, never glossy. Wide landscape composition (about 4:3), the subject readable at a glance.
Common people dress in simple wool and linen; lords in finer dyed cloth and mail.
