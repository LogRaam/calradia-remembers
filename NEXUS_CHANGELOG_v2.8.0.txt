Version 2.8.0

A letter box you sort yourself, tasks that say plainly what to do, characters who know their station and yours, what matters most to each of them, the world coming to you one at a time, and fixes from your reports.

New

- The letter box. A letter no longer opens a window when it arrives: you hear the courier, a notice names the sender, and the letter waits in the letter box, a new icon on the map bar beside the council's, with the number of letters waiting. Open it when you like. Letters are kept in order of arrival, marked when they look urgent, and with a ribbon when the sender is a correspondent you await. Throw a letter away and you will not hear of that subject from that sender again: you can accept it again at the bottom of the box. A war summons, a levy, a divorce or a child's support can be thrown away, but the game still holds you to them. (Thank you, tashmetu, for relaying the players buried in letters.)
- Letters are written when you open them, as of the day they were sent and from what their sender knew then. A letter thrown away or never opened costs no model call, so every letter a character means to send is sent: there is no more letter budget, and each sender's own wait between letters spaces a correspondence.
- Tasks say plainly what to do. Each task in the journal now has a short title written by the game ("Scout Sargot from inside (Dnin)") and a first line, "What to do:", that tells where to go, whom to see, what counts and what does not, whether the reward waits with the one who asked or is settled on the spot, and the deadline. The giver's own words stay underneath. Tasks already under way get it too. (Thank you, avalanche24.)
- Reply Length, in the MCM: Full (as always, 3 to 7 sentences), Balanced (2 to 4) or Short (1 to 3). (Thank you, RaveN017Mod.)
- Who is here, and who speaks: click the "Present: ..." line in the chat's title to open a panel of everyone in the room. Click a name to turn to them: a witness you turn to joins the conversation and speaks every turn, up to three voices at once. "Step back" returns someone to the witnesses. (Thank you, RaveN017Mod.)
- The world's initiative: lords seeking a word, companions, the household, tales, envoys and the law now take turns coming to you, one at a time, never in a battle or a conversation, with a quiet after a talk you started and variety across the month. An arrest, a liege's war summons, an ambush and a short-deadline envoy always pass. MCM group "The World's Initiative": how often the world comes to you, variety, and a weight per kind (Off keeps the old pacing).
- What matters most to each character: their children, their spouse, a lover, you, their family, their house's honour, wealth, power, glory, their lands, their freedom, or a score to settle. It weighs on what they agree to and forgive. Each may also have a fear, a superstition, and gifts they love or that leave them cold: a loved gift earns twice the regard. A new "What You Know" section in the encyclopedia names what you have learned of them.
- An awaited correspondence: click the ribbon on the letter book's left page, and that character's letters come first.
- Lords play their realm's strategy (MCM, Relationships, OFF by default, a first build): a realm grown too strong draws a counterweight from the others through the game's own decisions (allies called to arms, peace on other fronts, alliances, war), a house grown too strong draws plots, and a lord you know may ask you to strike the rival realm: beat it in the field, raid one of its villages or storm one of its strongholds.

Changed

- A parley with an enemy ruler, from the campaign bar: the council picker now offers "A parley with {realm}" for every realm at war with yours, when you are a ruler or head an independent house (peace, tribute, prisoners, a hostage, a non-aggression pact). (Thank you, timicu.)
- Companions know their place: a hired companion no longer speaks as a member of your house, knows you are their captain, and never orders you to prove yourself. (Thank you, avalanche24.)
- Every character knows where you stand beside them, in rank (a nobody, a sword for hire, the head of a rising house, a great lord, a sovereign) and at arms. (Thank you, avalanche24.)
- One definition per social station (ruler, lord, noble, companion, wanderer, merchant, artisan, landowner, headman, preacher, gang leader), each saying who they are and how they treat you at each rank. They are text files in ModuleData/stations, and you can override them in ModuleData/Custom/stations.
- The duel is a noble's affair; companions, wanderers, the street and the notables of trade settle a score with their fists instead, in a brawl where nobody dies. A commoner starts one only over a real grievance, and rarely against someone far stronger. (Thank you, avalanche24.)
- Tastes in bed (Hardcore only): thirteen more gestures, and each character's tastes now follow their nature and kinks. Tastes already rolled are kept and completed.
- A lord's friendly letter now waits for a deep bond, and lords no longer all write the same words. (Thank you, tashmetu.)

Fixed

- Give several goods at once, each in the amount you choose: the bag takes several goods and asks how many of each (Enter gives the whole stack). (Thank you, fearente.)
- A lord who asks for food takes any food, not only grain. (Thank you, fearente.)
- A companion's backstory no longer sends him after people who are not in the world: the people of his story belong to his past. (Thank you, fearente.)
- Characters know who the others in the room are to them (father, daughter, ruler of their realm, head of their house, governor) and their own children, without inventing any. Council seats too. (Thank you, fearente.)
- Lords no longer invent lands or wars: they are told which lands their house holds and which realms it fights, and that there are no others. (Thank you, RaveN017Mod.)
- Giving a ruler all you own no longer reads as joining her house: the window and the ruler now say you remain head of your house, sworn to her realm. (Thank you, RaveN017Mod.)

For modders

- CrAims: a lord now seeks the player out in person, or writes, about an aim your mod gave him.
- CrConception.RegisterVeto lets a mod forbid a conception CR would create (a sterile character, for one). (Thank you, tashmetu.)
- CrLetters.GetStatus answers "in_box" for a letter waiting unopened; throwing your mod's letter away raises CrLetters.SetAside.
- Removed from the MCM: "Letters from NPCs" and the initiative's "Weight: Letters" (the letter box replaces them).

Developer tools

- New tests: letter_box, quest_journal_plain, reply_length, participants_panel, initiative_director, initiative_turns, initiative_dials, matters_most, gift_tastes, parley_entry, player_rung, station_definitions, brawl_initiative, lord_strategy, strategist_aim, aims_channels, conception_veto, give_bundle, witness_standing; supply_quest, companion_identity and intimate_acts extended.

Notes

- Letters delivered before this version count as read; a letter on its way arrives in the box.
- Your existing saves load as they are.
