Version 2.8.6

Long campaigns load again: Calradia Remembers now keeps its records small in the save, and opens a save that an oversize record had already broken.

Fixed

- Long campaigns stopped loading with "An item with the same key has already been added". The game cannot load a save in which one block of data passes about 16.7 million bytes, and in a long campaign Calradia Remembers' memories and relation records could grow past that. They are now compressed before they go into the save, often to a tenth of their size, so they stay far under that limit and your saves get smaller too. If one ever grew close to the limit anyway, it would be left out of that save with a red warning rather than break the campaign. (Thank you, tashmetu, who found the cause.)
- A save already broken this way opens again. Load it with this version: the oversize block is left empty, a yellow notice tells you so, and you should save right away to keep the campaign loadable. What that block held (usually the record of how characters stand with each other, sometimes their memories) starts over. (Thank you, tashmetu.)
- An edict you decree through a governor or a lord is carried out even when the edict's name is worded the other way round ("requisition of grain", "muster of the militia"). Before, the edict was dropped.

Developer tools

- cr.save_sizes lists how large each Calradia Remembers record was at your last save, and how close it is to the limit.
- cr.save_rescue_probe arm writes an oversize block on purpose, to test the repair on a copy of a campaign (it adds about 320 MB to that save).
- cr.test all gains save_blobs.

Notes

- Existing saves load as they are, and a save broken by an oversize block opens again (see above).
- A save made with this version and then opened with an older version of Calradia Remembers loses the compressed records there (memories, relations between characters, jealousy and marriage approvals), and saving it with the older version makes that loss permanent. Keep this version once you have saved with it.
