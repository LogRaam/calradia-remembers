Version 2.6.5

Three fixes to the envoys who come to buy back a captive.

Fixed

- A captive is given away only when you say so. Before, accepting an envoy's final offer with something like "I will return him" could be taken as giving the captive for nothing. Now a plain yes closes the deal at the envoy's last offer, and only your own words ("free", "for nothing", "no ransom") make it a gift.
- The envoy no longer tells you the most their lord will pay. They haggle, and they may accept your figure if it is within what they were allowed.
- When an envoy offers an exchange of captives with gold added, they now explain that the gold evens out what the two captives are worth, so it no longer reads oddly beside the ransom offer. (Thank you, tashmetu, for all three.)

Notes

- Your existing saves load as they are.
