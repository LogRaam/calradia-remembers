Version 2.6.1

A small update: leaving a mercenary contract is now judged by the circumstances, as you asked.

New

- The end of a mercenary contract is judged by its circumstances. Leaving a realm at peace is no fault: that is how a mercenary works. Leaving a war the realm is winning, you go with thanks. Leaving a war it is losing, its lords remember that you took the pay and left them. Going over to its enemy soon after is remembered as a betrayal. Doing any of this to a realm of your own people weighs twice as much. And riding to the aid of your own people in a war they are losing is remembered well. Word of it gets about, in those words. (Thank you, fkasad and Zivkyn.)

- About being called an oathbreaker after leaving a mercenary contract: that came from how hiring on was recorded before 2.5.24 (as an oath of fealty). It was fixed then; news recorded before that update can still say so, and fades with time.

Developer tools

- Behind the scenes, the rules that read how characters regard you now say, in the code itself, whether they read Calradia Remembers' own regard or the game's relation, so the two can no longer be confused. Nothing changes in play.

Notes

- Your existing saves load as they are.
