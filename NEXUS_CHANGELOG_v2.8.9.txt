Version 2.8.9

Tasks you choose to accept, a new scouting task, characters who know the realms and the date, and a round of fixes from your reports on Nexus and Discord.

New

- A task is yours only if you accept it. When a character offers a task, a window now shows the deed, the reward and the deadline, and you choose Accept or Decline. Nothing goes into your journal until you accept, and declining costs you nothing. A character no longer hands you a task just because you spoke of an enemy, or because you asked them for a favour. (Thank you, Schmidt.)
- A new task: scout a place. A character or a war council can send you to ride within sight of a town, castle or village, and when you come back they read what you saw: who holds it, the garrison, the militia, the walls and how long its food lasts. The report comes from the game, never made up. (Thank you, bdionysis80.)
- A war council remembers what you said earlier in the sitting, and a long sitting no longer forgets everything older than a few turns.
- Every character knows the realms of Calradia, the towns and castles each holds, and which realms have fallen. (Thank you, Schmidt.)
- A lord knows the army he marches in: who leads it, which lords are in it and roughly how many men each brings. (Thank you, Schmidt.)
- A letter that changes a character's opinion of you now says so, with the same notice as a conversation. (Thank you, Schmidt.)
- The Docs folder has a guide to every prompt the mod sends: what each one is for, what triggers it, and which files in ModuleData you can edit to change it. (Thank you, CatWithFourEyes and Icezera.)

Fixed

- Characters on the Compact prompt for small models can give tasks again. With Compact on, they offer only the tasks the game has already checked against the world, so expect fewer than with the full prompt. (Thank you, superdeeplearningaibot.)
- Changing a character's "preferences" in character_overrides.json now takes effect after cr.reload_lore and in conversation. (Thank you, EvilPoptart1987.)
- Your spouse's letters speak of home, your children, their own days and the two of you. World news appears only when it touches your family, your house or you. (Thank you, Aporo.)
- A task that would mean attacking a realm you are at peace with now says so before you accept it, and giving it up while that peace holds costs no grudge and breaks no word. (Thank you, EvilPoptart1987.)
- Letters and conversations now carry the game's own date, so a letter is no longer dated a year ahead. (Thank you, bdionysis80.)
- A reply is no longer thrown away when your provider sends an empty usage detail, which some OpenAI-compatible providers do whenever the model did not reason. (Thank you, LapitaPolata.)
- When you hire someone, the confirmation shows the price you will really pay. (Thank you, Енот Василий.)
- A task the game cannot record now says why, to you and to the character, for example a castle offered as a reward while fief rewards are off in the settings. The character can then offer something else. (Thank you, Aporo.)
- A character only agrees to marry you when the game can actually perform the marriage. (Thank you, EmhyrTyl.)
- Coin paid to you for a captive you hand over now counts as the sale, so you are paid the agreed price.
- The Invite button no longer appears in a talk with an envoy, where an invited guest could never speak. (Thank you, Schmidt.)

Changed

- The Actions menu now also lists what you cannot do yet, greyed out, with the reason when you hover it: for example the regard a lord needs before leaving his house for yours. Asking a lord of another house to join your clan is now in the menu. (Thank you, Fakade.)
- Members of your own clan (your spouse, family and companions) no longer pay you for a task: it is your own money either way. They thank you with regard instead. Characters are also told not to pay you when you say you want no payment. (Thank you, Aporo.)

Developer tools

- cr.test all gains actions_menu_lord_join, clan_task_no_coin, council_sitting_memory, hire_price_shown, letter_date_real, override_preferences, quest_consent, quest_peace_warning, quest_refusal_reason, realms_and_armies and scout_place.

Notes

- Saves from 2.8.8 and older load as they are. A task already in your journal from an older version stays active.
