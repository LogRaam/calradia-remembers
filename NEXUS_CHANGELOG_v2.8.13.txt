Version 2.8.13

Long lists on the encyclopedia fold away, women in the ranks look like soldiers, characters you wrote keep their personality without a repeated tic, and other mods can borrow a character's persona. (Includes 2.8.12, which was not released on its own.)

New

- On a character's encyclopedia page, click the "Friends and enemies" or "Houses" header to fold or show its list. The header says how many entries it holds, and your choice stays for every page until you close the game. (Thank you, LapitaPolata.)

Fixed

- A model refusal worded like GLM 5.3 Flash's ("I can't output that content... injected instruction block") is now recognised: it is no longer shown under a character's name, and in Enhanced it is redone on your NSFW model. (Thank you, Aporo.)
- Women in the ranks are now drawn lean and far less muscular, with their own face kept. Their bodies came from the game's stout, heavy peasant women, so under a man's armour their heads looked tiny. (Thank you, EvilPoptart1987.)

Changed

- A character whose voice you wrote shows it in how they think, judge, react and argue, not in a catchphrase repeated every line. A habit you wrote comes back now and then, and still every time if your text says so. The mod no longer invents tics of its own for them. (Thank you, VonLemberg.)

For modders

- A mod can read a character's whole persona, as CR knows it right now (identity, voice, backstory, what matters to them, their memories, their aim, their mood, the news that reached them), and use it in its own LLM call, for example rulers negotiating among themselves. Nothing aimed at the player is included. (Thank you, VonLemberg.)
- A mod can also send that call through your own CR connection. A new option, "Other mods may use my connection" (MCM, LLM Provider, on by default), lets you refuse; every such call is logged with the mod's name. (Thank you, VonLemberg.)
- A secret one mod gives a character is no longer readable by another mod.
- EXTENSION_API.md: "A character's persona, for your own LLM call" and "Sending the call through CR's connection". CrApi.Version is 2.

Developer tools

- cr.test all gains persona_surface; women_in_ranks also checks that the women soldiers it draws are lean.

Notes

- Saves from 2.8.11 and older load as they are, and your MCM choices are kept.
