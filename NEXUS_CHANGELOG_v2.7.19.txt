Version 2.7.19

Love stories and lived aggressions in the encyclopedia, tales that ask for goods, new doors for other mods, and several fixes.

New

- Heart, in the encyclopedia: on the page of a character you court, love, married or parted from, the story of the bond is told to you ("She has been your secret lover since spring 1091"), with the wedding, when you became lovers and your children together. Your own page lists one line per such person. A bond an older save never dated is told without a date.
- Trauma, in the encyclopedia, at the Hardcore adult level only: on a character's page (never yours), the aggressions they lived through that you witnessed, told soberly. It starts with this version, so nothing from before shows.
- Tales can ask for goods and give an item: a stage can ask you to bring grain, horses, wine or a given item to someone, handed over with the bag (or written in the chat) in a conversation with them, and an ending can give you an item. Exactly the count asked leaves your party, and nothing leaves if you are short. (Thank you, tashmetu.)
- Four new conditions and outcomes for tale writers: your character's sex, a notable's trade, a town as the place of a tale, and gold you pay in an ending. (Thank you, tashmetu.)

Fixed

- Five deeds are now fully taught to the character who can perform them, not only listed: arranging a match (including a groom's side asking you for your daughter's or sister's hand), reinforcing a garrison, sharing intelligence, delivering recruits, and confessing a secret love. Characters use them far more reliably. (Thank you, tashmetu.)
- A character no longer asks you the same question again after a companion or bystander speaks; the question waits for your answer. (Thank you, Lordfadooboo.)
- Kingdom votes: the mod's code that runs when a vote is decided can no longer throw an error into the game; any fault is written to the log instead. (Thank you, CarolusIV.)
- A tale's giver no longer remembers the same ending twice. (Thank you, tashmetu.)
- Another mod's secret memories no longer show in the chat's Memories panel before they come to light. A new MCM option, Show Secret Memories (Relationships, off by default), lists them marked as secret. (Thank you, tashmetu.)
- On the encyclopedia, a character's friends and enemies are now headed "Friends and enemies, and why:", and the houses at odds or of affinity are shown as titled lists.

For modders

- A regard source can be registered as a plain function, so a mod that keeps Calradia Remembers optional still loads without it (CrPairRegard.RegisterPairRegardSource). (Thank you, tashmetu.)
- A letter your mod delivers now has an id you can follow: its status, the player's reply, and events when it is answered or set aside (CrLetters.DeliverWithId, Replied, SetAside, GetStatus, GetReply). (Thank you, tashmetu.)
- Lords can be given aims of their own by another mod, which they raise with the player in conversation (CrAims.Register, Retract, Raised). (Thank you, tashmetu.)
- A match for a woman of the player's house can be proposed with the groom's side as the decider; the player is asked for her hand and confirms in the chat. (Thank you, tashmetu.)
- A mercenary contract ended by the employer can be reported, so it no longer counts as the player abandoning the realm (CrMercenaries.ContractEndedByEmployer). (Thank you, tashmetu.)
- See EXTENSION_API.md and HOWTO_Quests.md for the details.

Developer tools

- New cr.testall tests: heart_page, trauma_page, tale_items, tale_keys, tale_locals_memory, aims_door, match_asks_the_player, mercenary_dismissed, memory_panel_secrets, every_verb_taught, mcm_test_buttons_wired; pair_regard_source, letter_from_another_mod, registered_match and admit_secret_lover extended.
- The MCM Test buttons now write what they did and the result to the log.

Notes

- Your existing saves load as they are.
