Version 2.8.2

Lords who talk with their prisoner, extortion and secrets you can tell, legends, friends who spare your villages, calls for help by letter, and prices that match what changes hands.

New

- A lord who holds you prisoner can send for you to talk, at every content level, not only at Hardcore. Depending on his nature and on what lies between you, he may offer courtesy, a ransom you can pay on the spot, your freedom on your word not to attack his realm for 60 days, an exchange for one of his people you hold, or simply keep you. Breaking your word has a cost; keeping it earns a little regard. Setting: Captor Conversations, on. (Thank you, Windmolen.)
- You can extort a character, but only over a hold the game can see: an affair you know of, or one of their villages within reach of men well beyond theirs. They decide before they speak whether to pay, from their nature, the hold and their rank. Paying or refusing costs their regard and leaves a grudge, and it goes on your Misdeeds page. Coin taken to spare lands you then raid is remembered as a betrayal.
- You can tell a secret you hold: your own married lover, or a partner's hidden lover found out. It becomes a scandal that costs every house in it renown and influence, yours included, and those it names remember it. A secret told is spent: nobody holds it over you any more.
- Lords have secrets of their own. Now and then a married lord takes a hidden lover. Nothing shows unless you send a companion to spy on that lord by name: a good errand reports it, a poor one says nothing certain, and a lord with nothing to hide is reported clean. Once you know, you can extort them over it or tell it. Needs Adult Content above Off. Setting: Hidden Affairs Among Lords, on.
- Legends: a battle won against three to one, an assault thrown back at those odds, or a last stand that cost the enemy dearly becomes a named legend. Word travels across Calradia, and each character tells it their own way, by their bond to it, with the facts kept straight. Yours and the lords' alike. Setting: Legends, on. (Thank you, DepaHN.)
- Your raids on villages and the towns and castles you sack now go on your Misdeeds page, with a softer line for a raid in a war already under way.
- A friend or a relative at war with you may spare your villages. The deeper the friendship, the likelier; never certain, never under a vendetta. Raiding his lands or killing one of his house ends it until the peace. The closest of them tells you so by letter, once per war. Sieges are unchanged. Setting: Friends May Spare Your Villages, on. (Thank you, DepaHN.)
- Calls for help by letter: friends, kin and allies in real trouble (a siege, a lost battle, a ruined house, a fief lost, a town out of food) write to ask for relief, food or coin. You answer with the letter's buttons, and what you then do counts more than what you answered: help that comes is thanked, a promise broken or a plea ignored is remembered, and you owe nothing if you truly could not come. Arriving at a besieged place you were called to brings it food and militia. (Thank you, DepaHN.)

Fixed

- A married character now says they cannot marry you, instead of agreeing and leaving the game to refuse "under the laws of Calradia". This was not Stolen Love. (Thank you, EmhyrTyl.)
- A lord can ride in your party while his realm is at war. He says as he agrees that his liege may call him away, and leaves, with a notice, when his clan's leader or his ruler leads an army, unless you march in that same army. (Thank you, Lordfadooboo and Maifailun.)
- A task a model wrote in the wrong form is now given when it can be read, and when it cannot you are told "No task was recorded" and the character is asked to state it again. (Thank you, avalanche24.)
- When the Actions Interpreter's online provider is busy, the log now says so instead of blaming your model's context size. (Thank you, avalanche24.)
- On the encyclopedia page of someone who is not the head of their house, the Houses section says whose standing it shows: "Houses (Banu Azad, through Shaykh Yasir):". (Thank you, tashmetu.)
- At Hardcore, a lord's ransom offer reaches you again. (Thank you, Windmolen.)
- The letters the mod writes itself (threats, warnings, a friend's favour, blackmail, a mother's note, a companion's farewell) are now translated into your Reply Language when you open them. (Thank you, RaveN017Mod.)

Changed

- What a character says about gold now matches what you receive. Characters know what things are worth in your game and what their purse holds. A gift for nothing is a share of their purse by their regard for you, a sale stops at three times an item's value, a ransom at what the captive fetches, and a sworn debt is paid in full. The sum in the line you read, in the memory and in the payment is the same. When less is paid than was named, the notice says why. (Thank you, RaveN017Mod.)
- A lord no longer applauds every companion who speaks up: he judges what was said by his own regard for them, and may agree, disagree or put them in their place. (Thank you, froggyluv.)
- Characters recite the past less and keep small things small. A character now knows what it has already told you about a memory, and does not bring it up again at every meeting unless you raise it or something new happens. (Thank you, kurushimi00.)

Developer tools

- cr.affairs, cr.extortions [name], cr.legends, cr.legend <name>, cr.raid_restraint, cr.help_pleas and cr.help_plea <name> [coin|food] show or drive the new systems.
- cr.test all gains hidden_affairs, secret_exposure, extortion, legends, misdeeds_page, raid_restraint, help_plea, captor_terms, memory_discussed, coin_matches_words and fixed_letter_translation; ride_with_me now also covers the muster.

Notes

- The four new settings (Captor Conversations, Hidden Affairs Among Lords, Legends, Friends May Spare Your Villages) are on by default and can be turned off in the mod options.
- Raids and sacks made before this version are not on the Misdeeds page.
- Existing saves load as they are.
