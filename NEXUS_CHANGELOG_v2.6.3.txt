Version 2.6.3

A fix for ransoms paid in conversation.

Fixed

- When a lord buys back a captive you hold, the ransom is now paid once, and the figure is the real one. Before, a lord could promise a sum they did not have, the game paid what they could, and then tried to pay a second time from an empty purse; the lord could even take that failed payment as a theft and hold it against you. Now a lord knows, before naming a price, the most they can actually pay; if less is paid than the figure named, you are told why; and a ransom is never paid twice. (Thank you, tashmetu.)

Notes

- Your existing saves load as they are. A memory already formed from this bug stays; new ones will not form.
